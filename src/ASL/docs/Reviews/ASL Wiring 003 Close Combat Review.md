## Memory and performance

The caller reads only the participating units' facts. Concealment uses its existing indexed traversal. Search reuses its selected enemies; Mop Up reuses its selected defenders. Ambush scans state units once for participant designations and reads indexed stacks for experience. No new persistent cache, map rebuild, or retained game graph is introduced. Recorded properties are nullable and omitted when absent. No performance improvement is claimed.

Since the review of PR 80: concealment reads a unit's Stealth status only when it needs a dr (a `Func`, as the old Inexperience was), so a Player Turn's end no longer gathers every unit's status up front. Search and Mop Up each read every active unit's status once before their dice (one pass over the state's units: a group lookup, an SSR scan, and an experience read apiece); both are commands a player gives a few times a turn, and the pass allocates nothing that outlives it.

## Review of PR 80 settled (2026-10-31)

- **Item 27, a refusal that depended on a hidden fact.** Search and Mop Up refused after the dr when a concealed or hidden defender's Stealth status was unknown, which told the searcher a real squad or HS was under the "?" and let the dr be drawn again. Both now ask for every active unit's Stealth facts, both sides, before any dice and before any "?" is read (`GamePlanner.StealthStateRefusal`); the refusal names no unit and does not depend on what is searched. The checks inside the dice remain only as guards (`KnownStealth`). Backlog 87 records the disclosure, its settlement, and what remains (a setup check would also cover the vehicle's "inexperienced" precheck).
- **Item 28, old members.** Removed: `ConcealmentCandidateFacts.Lax`, `.Stealthy`, `.Heroic`, and `.Inexperienced`; the hero tests and Lax facts of `SearcherFacts` and `MopUpDefenderFacts` (`Hero`, `Heroic`, `Lax`) and the null-Stealth fallbacks of `ScenarioA1Searching.Drm`, `ScenarioA1MoppingUp.CasualtyDrm`, and `ScenarioA1FireFollowUps`; the old `ConcealmentGains` entry point (its only callers were tests); and the no-context `ScenarioA1VehicleCloseCombat.Resolve` overload, which did not give the context reading on every input (an Unarmed or swimming attacker, a berserk attacker's leader DRM, a Final DR equal to a CCV of 0 or less). `Stealth` is required on all three facts records (a lazy read on the concealment candidate, a known status on the Search and Casualty facts). The recorded reading stays reachable by an explicit null context, which the verifier passes for a record with no `ccvVersion`. The Search and Casualty drms are recorded in their events and the projector does not recompute them, so no recorded game's answer changes.
- **Item 29, rules decided in Play.** `ScenarioA1CloseCombatValueCalculator.NationalCcvOf` chooses the national CCV case from the nationality; `AssaultEngineerLostByRally` and `AssaultEngineerLostByRearming` decide the AE loss of a Commissar's rally (A25.222, p. 95) and of a rearmed prisoner (A20.551, p. 88).
- **RA11C.9 (new ruling).** A Commissar's failed rally loses Assault Engineer status only when it Replaces the unit by the next lower quality unit of its size; when the unit is already the lowest quality and is Casualty Reduced instead (A25.222), that is no "Replaced by a lower quality unit" (A25.01, p. 93), so the lineage records no loss. The old caller recorded a loss in that case too. No projected answer differs: an Assault Engineer is Elite (A25.01) and never the lowest quality, and for a unit with no AE parent a recorded `false` and `true` project the same status. A newly written lineage of that kind now carries `assaultEngineerLoss: false`.
- **Item 30.** The concealment read is lazy (above). The second gatherer of Inexperience and the missing Play tests of `CcvFromState` are backlog 87 rows, with why. The SSR fold's second copy waits for PR 79's member, as the status line says.
