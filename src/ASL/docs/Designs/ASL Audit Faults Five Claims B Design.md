## 0. How the five claims fix faults that are wrong in play

A rule claim changes no caller (AGENTS.md, step 2), and an old Rules member keeps its answer unless the new one is equal on every input (the Rules conventions, section 2, step 5). So in each claim:

- The new calculator gives the rule's answer, every fault included, and its tests prove it.
- Each old member that gives the same answer on every input forwards to it.
- Each old member whose answer is wrong keeps it, and its summary names the calculator member that replaces it and the difference.
- The wiring row `W-<claim>` lists every site that must move, in Play, the projector, the Studio, Maps, and the Rules sites that kept their answers, with the behavior change each brings. Codex's wiring claims move them, with older recorded games kept on their recorded reading, and run the merge gate.

## 1. Claim D5.34: Stun and Encirclement (rows D5.34, D5.342, A7.7, A7.71, A7.72)

### 1.1 The rule (D5.34 p. 203; D5.342 p. 204; D5.311, D5.33 p. 203; D5.341, D5.343, D5.6 p. 204; A14.3 p. 82; A9.61; B24.121; A7.7 p. 57; A7.71, A7.72 p. 58; A10.532 pp. 67 and 68; A20.21 p. 87; B30.32 p. 150; the C5 chart pp. 173 and 189; the IFT DRM list p. 687)

Stun:
- An AFV's Inherent crew is Stunned, and the AFV marked, in four cases: (a) a CE crew that qualifies for its normal CE DRM fails a MC (an Unprotected crew, D5.311, is "not susceptible to Stun/Recall"); (b) the CE crew of a fully armored AFV takes a Sniper dr 2 (A14.3); (c) a MG's Final TK DR equals the Final TK#, even against a BU AFV (A9.61); (d) Falling Rubble lands on a CE CT AFV (B24.121).
- D5.342: a crew that suffers a second Stun "(e.g., while already under the effects of a +1 counter)" suffers Recall instead. The "e.g." makes the front (Stunned) side a first Stun too.
- While Stunned, for the rest of the Player Turn: the AFV and its Passengers become BU at once and may not regain CE until D5.33 allows it in a later Player Turn; it may not fire (CC included), move, change CA, or spend MP; it Stops at once.
- Stun +1, the back side, from the end of the Player Turn it was placed in: the AFV adds 1 to "any TH, MG/IFE/FT IFT, CC, TC/MC, Crew Survival, or OVR DR it makes", and nothing else. The counter stays with the crew if it Abandons.
- D5.341 (the 1MT Recall, Recall's back side) and D5.343 (a SMC Temporary crew's wound) take this claim's verdict as a fact; they are not this claim's rows. A10.532 bars Interdiction by armament "currently penalized by the +1 BU or +1 Stun DRM".

Encirclement:
- A7.7: any non-Aerial Infantry, or the Vulnerable PRC of an Immobile vehicle, fired on consecutively in one PFPh, DFPh, or AFPh by two or more non-Aerial units. Never a pillbox or its contents (B30.32).
- Valid fire: inherent FP, SW, ordnance, or vehicular armament, at Normal Range, which A10.532 caps at 16 hexes. Ordnance must hit; other fire must be able to inflict at least a NMC, Cowering counted. Fire at another target between the attacks breaks the sequence.
- Geometry: the LOS enter the Location through opposite hexspines, with three vertices between them both ways, or through three non-contiguous hexsides; or a LOF from directly above and directly below in a building. A7.71: a FG counts each hexside its LOS cross, and one FG may Encircle alone.
- Effects: every non-berserk, non-heroic enemy (or Melee) Personnel unit there drops one morale level against the sealing attack and every later attack on the Location; "All fire by an Encircled unit is subject to a +1 DRM on the IFT (or To Hit DR if ordnance)"; the first Location an Encircled unit enters costs double MF after all modification. The marker stands until all those units leave, become berserk or heroic, are eliminated, or are captured.
- A7.72: a non-heroic, non-berserk unit in an upper building Location is Encircled while it cannot trace a path to ground level free of an unbroken, armed, unconcealed enemy unit or a Blaze.
- What each side knows: Stun counters, the Encirclement marker, the rolls, and the attack's facing are public; under a concealed or hidden AFV's "?" its counters are the owner's; a concealed unit's kind is the owner's; A7.72 counts only unconcealed enemies.

## 8. Questions for the user

1. **D5.34: one claim in two increments, in its narrow form.** Stun (D5.34, D5.342) and Encirclement (A7.7 to A7.72) share no fact; they meet only in the ordnance To Hit list, which is the survey's fault. As one claim it holds about 29 files, most shared for a one-line forward or a nullable field; each calculator has about ten members. The wider form would add `StunnedThisPlayerTurn`, one predicate for the "may not fire, move, or regain CE" test repeated in `Halted`, `KeepMovingStack`, `MotionAttempts.Why`, and `VehicleProjection.Recalling`, at the cost of three more held files; those bars are right today and only read the condition. **Proposal:** one claim, built in two increments (Stun, then Encirclement), each with its own calculator, contract, tests, and bundle, in the narrow form (RD5.1). The repeated test stays where it is, and its merge goes to the D5.341 claim, which owns Recall's share of it.
2. **A20.54: the double CVP and Unarmed units that are not prisoners.** A20.54 says "Prisoners/Unarmed units eliminated by fire from their own side still count double". Read alone, it would give double CVP for any Unarmed unit. But "still" refers to units that already count double, and only captured units do (A20.1, A26.222); A26.222, the CVP rule itself, grants the double only "if a prisoner is eliminated due to an attack by its own side"; and an Unarmed unit that is no longer guarded (abandoned, or freed and not recaptured) is not captured, so its captor holds no CVP for it to double. **Proposal:** no; the double applies only to a unit captured when it is eliminated, and an Unarmed non-prisoner counts normal CVP (RA20.3).
3. **E1.8: is an Opportunity Fire declaration a Gunflash?** E1.8's list of counters that mark a Gunflash names "Opportunity Fire", but its premise is "any unit that fires", and an A7.25 declaration places a Bounding Fire counter on a unit that has not fired. The code has always read the counter as a Gunflash. E1.91 opens the first Starshell only on "an attack vs an enemy unit", and a declaration is no attack. **Proposal:** the declared unit's Location is a Gunflash for E1.8's sight (the counter list is explicit, and no game changes), marked from the `OpportunityFireDeclared` event (W-E1.8), and it does not open the first Starshell (RE1.4). The letter and the premise disagree, so this is the user's to veto.
4. **B23.74: older Tractor Works games, and the Maps repaint.** Every recorded game carries the SSR tokens it started with (`GameStarted.SpecialRules`); no card SHA is recorded in the game start. A game recorded before the card's SSR 5 gains `factory:X3` lacks the token, so keying the Factory reading on the recorded token keeps older games on the stone-building reading with no SHA and no new version property, and the batch gate's replay and sweep of `dl-tractor-works-02` show no difference. Painting X3 as a 2½-level Factory and the two new `LosResult` facts are Maps work, which no rule claim may do; without them the Rules verdicts are right but every LOS inside a building still blocks. **Proposal:** key the reading on the recorded SSR token (RB23F.14), and put the repaint and the LOS facts in W-B23.74 as Maps work for track 3, applied only when the recorded token is present. The research's alternatives (the card's SHA, a version) are not needed.
5. **C10.3: lift ruling R8.6's limit of pushes to Open Ground or grain?** R8.6 (the Backlog Passes Plan, pass 8) pushes a Gun only into an adjacent Open Ground or grain hex at its level. C10.3 allows any terrain at its TEM and MF (its EX counts +4 for the MF of a stone building), and bars marsh (B16.41), deep streams, Bamboo, upper levels, and large Guns in buildings (B23.423). The new DRM takes `HexTem`, so the arithmetic is ready. Lifting the limit needs more: the wiring must gather the hex's TEM and the Gun's target size, `PushRefusal` must take B16.41 and B23.423 as facts, and the C2.7 row notes that "Pushing is limited to Open Ground and grain by R8.6, so only setup can break it": once lifted, a push could also take a Gun where C2.7 forbids one. **Proposal:** keep R8.6 in this claim (RC10.8): the old members keep its refusal and text, the calculator decides only C10.3's own bars, and W-C10.3 lists lifting it as an optional behavior change for the user's word at wiring, together with B16.41's and B23.423's bars and a C2.7 check.
