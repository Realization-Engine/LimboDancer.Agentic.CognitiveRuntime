## 1. Rulebook evidence and boundaries

Physical PDF pages refer to eASLRB v3.01. All 716 pages were searched for D2.321, D2.33 and their Bypass-facing wording. Pages 197 and 198 were read together; the page 197 examples were also inspected as a rendered image.

| Source | Consequence for this design |
|---|---|
| D2.32 and D2.321, p. 197 (D6) | Bypass has a CAFP and four turret positions: front, rear and two distinct sides. Its front/rear regions are based on the hexes beyond the respective vertices, not an ordinary hex-center CA. A side TCA includes the intervening side region. |
| D2.321 example, p. 197 | ST front-to-side costs +3; switching to a different target within that unchanged side costs +2; firing again with applicable acquired-target DRM costs no Case A; front-to-rear through a side costs +4. | C5.1, p. 171; C5.11-C5.13, p. 172 | Reuse the existing Rules Case A rate for the weapon, substituting Target Facing steps for hexspines. Keep terrain doubling and other fire composition separate. Bounding First Fire cannot turn as part of its shot and does not incur Case A. |

| D2.33, pp. 197-198 (D6-D7) | At a CAFP, a VCA turn selects one of the two incident hexsides other than the occupied lane and costs 1 MP. The vehicle cannot enter the obstacle it is currently bypassing, pivot merely to shoot, or voluntarily finish its MPh between that turn and the required continuation. |
| D2.33 continuation, p. 198 | Before completion, Defensive First Fire and immobilization use the CAFP and Target Facing occupied before the turn. Continuation is to the next CAFP, or Reverse into a new rear hex. |

| C.5B-C.5C, p. 162 | Arc membership must contain the target's occupied hex, not merely touch its aiming vertex. The obstacle hex remains the occupied hex for a Bypass target. |
| B31.11-B31.123, p. 152; vehicle movement chart, pp. 220/690 | A Narrow Street halves the normal Bypass entry expenditure, but its D2.33 VCA turn remains 1 MP. Narrow Street turret restrictions and their dr are separate prerequisites, not waived by D2.321 geometry. |

The design supplies the D2.32 geometry needed by these two rules, not a complete replacement of LOS, armor facing or C.5 boundary resolution. Same-hex fire and incoming fire originating at a CAFP are explicitly not evaluated in the defensive-facing query until the appropriate C.5 calculation is composed. An explicit origin-kind fact distinguishes them from supported hex-center fire. They must not fall back to ordinary hex-center geometry. D2.32's full audit and special-case firing remain separate work; these limits are recorded in backlog section 102.

## 3. Shared position model

The D2.321 models file owns the small shared immutable `BypassFrameFacts` / `BypassFrame` types. D2.33 consumes them without a second geometry implementation. Facts contain the bypassed hex, across-lane hex and front-vertex third hex as integer axial coordinate pairs in one normalized board frame. Labels such as `[BB3]` are presentation identifiers, not geometry. The rear vertex is derived. Validate distinct adjacent hexes and a genuine common vertex before producing a frame.

Maps adapters later supply coordinates and orientation already present in map facts; Rules determines adjacency, frame validity and sectors. Rotated boards and seams must be normalized before calling. Missing or incompatible coordinate frames are not evaluated. No pixel geometry, map/state object or hidden-unit lookup enters Rules.

The normalized axial basis is fixed: `(1,0)` points north-east at 30 degrees, `(0,1)` north at 90 degrees, and the remaining neighbors are `(-1,1)`, `(-1,0)`, `(0,-1)`, `(1,-1)`. This matches the existing east-at-zero facing convention; it is not a screen-coordinate convention. For orientation reasoning, Cartesian coordinates are proportional to `(sqrt(3)*q, q+2*r)`, with north positive. Implementation uses integer transforms, not floating-point bearings. A canonical frame has obstacle `(0,0)`, across-lane hex `(0,1)`, front apex `(1,0)` and derived rear apex `(-1,1)`, facing east. Its front sector is `(1+a+b,-b)` and rear sector `(-1-a-b,1+b)` for nonnegative integers `a,b`. Of remaining hexes, `q+2*r>1` is Left and `<1` is Right. Rotate/translate the frame to this basis for classification; reflection swaps sides. Tests must fix these expected regions independently of the classifier and also reproduce the printed example.

`BypassTurretPosition` has `Front`, `Right`, `Rear`, `Left`, in clockwise order viewed along the frame. The frame determines which side contains the bypassed obstacle. Do not collapse the two sides into the existing string `side`, or store these values as `UnitFacing` 0-5.

Geometry methods on `ScenarioA1BypassTurretCalculator`:

- `Frame(facts)`: validate and construct the occupied frame.
- `Sector(frame, occupiedTargetHex)`: classify a known hex into one of the four regions using the D2.32 diagram's hex regions, with integer hex arithmetic and inclusive bounding rows. Returning a region says nothing about LOS through the obstacle.
- `TurretChange(from, to, direction)`: validate an explicitly selected clockwise/counterclockwise path and return Target Facing steps and whether it enters or passes through a side. An omitted direction selects the unique shortest path; for opposite positions it returns not-evaluated with a direction-required reason. Equal positions require no direction and mean zero steps; a direction with equal positions is an unsupported full revolution, not zero history. Reject contradictory path facts. An explicit direction may select a one-, two- or three-step path.

Every entry point has an explicit nullable `inBypass` context describing the initial occupied position (also the current position for a query without a movement transition). On branches needing that position, false is not applicable, null is unknown, and true with a missing required frame is not evaluated. The movement calculator receives an immutable **last occupied frame**, a **pending VCA turn**, and, after actual entry, a **completed position**. The latter is a tagged choice: a new Bypass frame, or an ordinary occupied hex and VCA when forward/Reverse movement leaves Bypass. In `DefensivePosition` at `EntryCompleted`, dispatch by completed-position kind first: require only that position's facts, not the old frame or initial `inBypass` value. Do not invent a Bypass frame for an outright exit. A pending turn never overwrites the old frame. While pending, loss of the old frame is unknown information, not permission to reconstruct it from the turned VCA.

## 4. D2.321 calculator

Proposed files: `ScenarioA1BypassTurretModels.cs`, `ScenarioA1BypassTurretCalculator.cs`, `ScenarioA1BypassTurretContract.cs`.

`FireAdjustment` takes frame and occupied-target-hex facts (and derives the sector itself), current and proposed turret positions, turn direction, weapon traverse profile, fire mode, weapon category and the applicable acquisition or repeated-target facts. Bounding First Fire also needs the nullable fact `earlierMpTcaChange` for this MPh. It returns disposition (`Evaluated`, `Refused`, `NotApplicable`, `NotEvaluated`), selected position, step count, normal Case A component, separate Bypass side-change **To Hit** DRM, reason and citations. It is an arc/DRM component, not complete firing permission. The To Hit surcharge is not automatically an IFT DRM or FP multiplier.

1. Non-Bypass is not applicable. A bow/non-turreted weapon is not a turret case; a request to use these turret positions for it is refused. Unknown/unsupported profiles never take the legacy NT default.
2. The final position must contain the target's occupied hex. Obstacle LOS and other firing restrictions are independent verdicts. No same-hex or unresolved boundary case is silently accepted.
3. A changed TCA pays the existing C5.1 rate for **Target Facing steps**, plus one additional DRM if the selected path enters or passes through either side. The extra is one per turn path, not one per side encountered. Starting in a side and turning directly to front/rear does not by itself enter or pass through a side.
4. With unchanged front/rear TCA, this rule adds zero. With unchanged side TCA, ordinary Case A is the rate for one step even though the actual step count is zero; the side-change surcharge is zero.
5. The unchanged-side Case A is waived for ordnance using an already-earned applicable Target Acquisition DRM. CMG/IFE/Canister instead require the same Known target in the same Target Facing as that weapon last fired on; acquisition does not substitute for that category-specific test. A mere acquisition counter somewhere, target identity alone, or another weapon's history is insufficient. Unknown facts in a needed exception do not become false or true; return not-evaluated unless another known branch settles it.
6. The exception applies only within the current TCA. It does not waive a real turret turn. Non-ordnance FP conversion and D3.51 maintained modifiers are not performed here. The result is a local component, not a total: the current ordinary-turn composer cannot be assumed to combine it correctly. Bypass maintained-history and IFT composition must be settled in Rules before wiring those paths, never by arithmetic in Play. Keep these paths unwired until then.
7. Bounding First Fire is a separate mode: a change as part of the shot is refused under C5.13. With unchanged TCA and `earlierMpTcaChange=false`, the local Case A and side-change components are zero. With `earlierMpTcaChange=true`, this slice returns not-evaluated pending the separate +1/history composition ruling; with null, it returns not-evaluated for missing history. Do not turn a Case A exemption into an assumed exemption from the chart's separate Other DRM. Other modes do not require this fact. This verdict does not authorize an MP action or waive other Bounding First Fire penalties.
8. Normal Case A terrain multiplication is not the Bypass surcharge. Return them separately for Rules composition. This slice covers ordinary Bypass, with no woods/building/rubble doubling at the actual firing point. A special Bypass position that is itself in such terrain is explicitly not evaluated pending the C5.11 composition review, rather than applying an unverified multiplier.

Examples required for approval and implementation: ST front-to-side = normal 2 plus side 1; ST front-to-rear through side = normal 3 plus side 1; unchanged ST side at a new target = normal 2 plus side 0; unchanged side with applicable acquisition = 0; side-to-front ST = normal 2 plus side 0. Fast traverse uses the existing rate, not ST's rate. An unsupported traverse type is not evaluated.

Accepted traverse profiles are exactly the existing `t`, `st`, `rst`, `1mt` turret profiles; `nt` and bow-mounted weapons are not turret cases. Categories distinguish ordinary ordnance, CMG, IFE and Canister; do not infer category from whether an acquisition fact happens to be present. The same-target/same-facing exception is conditional on the latter three categories; ordinary ordnance requires applicable earned acquisition for that exception.

## 5. D2.33 calculator

Proposed files: `ScenarioA1BypassVcaModels.cs`, `ScenarioA1BypassVcaCalculator.cs`, `ScenarioA1BypassVcaContract.cs`. These reuse the frame types and geometry above.

| Method | Facts and verdict |
|---|---|
| `TurnAtCafp` | Occupied frame, nullable pending-turn state, requested incident hexside and purpose (`ContinueMovement` or `FireOnly`). Refuse FireOnly, a second pending turn, the occupied lane and any non-incident edge. A valid local turn returns exactly 2 half-MP (1 MP), proposed VCA and a continuation obligation; it does not certify the entry's terrain legality or available MP. |
| `Continuation` | Occupied frame, pending turn, candidate completed-position kind, destination/optional straddled edge/new CAFP and forward/reverse mode. Forward after a turn must follow its selected connecting lane to the next CAFP. Reverse must enter a new rear hex under the requested movement direction and may produce an ordinary position rather than a new Bypass frame. Refuse entry into the currently bypassed obstacle and returning along the just-traversed lane as forward continuation. Validate geometry in Rules rather than accepting a caller's `isLegalContinuation` boolean. |
| `EndMovement` | Takes `endCause` (`Voluntary`, `Immobilized`, `OtherForcedHalt`) and, for immobilization, its known Rules-resolved fact. Voluntary end with a pending turn and no completed continuation is refused, even with no affordable option. Known immobilization produces a forced-end disposition; `DefensivePosition` separately supplies the position that must be retained. Unknown immobilization is not evaluated; other forced halts are explicitly unsupported pending their own Rules composition. A Stop expenditure is not itself an end of MPh: reverse movement may require Stop/Start, and those actions do not clear the obligation. Their legality/cost remains with existing Rules. |
| `DefensivePosition` | For Defensive First Fire or immobilization before continuation completes, return the previous occupied CAFP/frame and its Target Facing basis, not the newly proposed VCA. At completed Bypass entry use the new frame. At a completed outright exit return the ordinary position with a not-applicable Bypass-facing disposition; existing ordinary-facing Rules then apply. If immobilized while pending, retain the prior frame as the final occupied defensive position; no movement is invented. |

Without a pending turn, forward exit into the hex forming the VCA base and an outright Reverse exit are supported local candidates. Reverse into a Bypass lane without a pending turn is not-evaluated: D2.22 p. 196 permits qualifying Reverse Bypass, but its general geometry is separate from this post-turn D2.33 slice. D2.3/2.31 clearance, D2.11/2.13/2.21 costs, MP budget, Bog, ESB, enemy-AFV restrictions and movement windows remain prerequisites supplied by their existing Rules calculators. A successful D2.33 component is not permission to ignore them. The two permitted turn choices must be derived from the three hexes incident at the CAFP, not merely any adjacent numeric facing.

The 1-MP VCA turn is a separately announced expenditure (D2.11, p. 195). It is not part of the entry cost multiplied under D2.21 (p. 196). Keep turn cost, the later forward/Reverse entry cost and any Stop/Start costs separate; this calculator neither computes a Reverse multiplier nor charges the turn twice.

**Pending-turn Reverse ruling, derived from D2.22/D2.32/D2.33 rather than a printed example:** name the three hexes at the old CAFP `A` (bypassed obstacle), `B` (across the lane) and `F` (front apex). The selected connecting lane is A-F or B-F, and the proposed movement VCA points away from that old CAFP along the selected lane. Reverse travels back through the old CAFP into its third incident hex, the one not in the selected lane. A-F therefore gives B; B-F gives A, which is refused because it is the currently bypassed obstacle. For the canonical coordinates in section 3, A-F proposes VCA 5 (south-east) and Reverse destination `(0,1)`; B-F proposes VCA 1 and its Reverse destination `(0,0)` is refused. Test both choices and all rotations/reflections. Reverse eligibility and any required Stop/Start are separate existing Rules prerequisites. This is movement geometry: it must not replace the old A-B/F defensive frame before actual entry. Without a pending turn, a Reverse outright exit uses the rear apex of the occupied frame instead.

Legacy Bypass portions of `TurnBar`, `MoveOptions`, `MayMoveOn`, `StopBar` and `EndMoveBar` move behind `LegacyTurnRefusal`, `LegacyMoveOptions`, `LegacyMayMoveOn`, `LegacyStopRefusal` and `LegacyEndMoveRefusal` compatibility helpers in the new calculator. Existing public signatures, callback order, first-refusal text and answers remain unchanged; unrelated rule checks stay in their existing order. In particular, legacy Reverse omission and the conditional stop/end behavior are preserved for old callers. The new API implements the corrected reading. No new field is added to serialized `OrdnanceShot`, movement events or game state in this claim.

## 6. Contracts and disclosure

Register separate small contracts rather than one union of unrelated required facts:

| Contract | Inputs and outputs |
|---|---|
| `d2.321-bypass-tca` | Frame coordinate pairs, target occupied-hex coordinates, turret positions/direction, weapon profile/category, fire mode, special firing-point terrain flag, applicable acquisition, same Known target, same facing, conditional `earlierMpTcaChange`. Output sector, target-facing step count and the two DRM components, or a refusal/not-evaluated reason. |
| `d2.33-bypass-turn` | Frame, pending turn, requested connecting edge, movement/fire-only purpose. Output permitted local turn, 2 half-MP, proposed VCA and continuation required. |
| `d2.33-bypass-continuation` | Action (`Entry` or `EndMovement`), frame, pending turn/selected edge, destination and completed-position kind (Bypass frame or ordinary hex/VCA), forward/reverse; for an end action, `endCause` and conditional known immobilization. Output permitted component, continued obligation, forced-end, refusal or not-evaluated. End actions do not require prospective destination geometry; entry actions do not require an end cause. |
| `d2.33-bypass-defensive-position` | Old frame, optional completed position and kind, stage (`AtOccupiedPosition`, `TurnPending`, `EntryCompleted`), separate nullable immobilized flag, origin kind (hex center or CAFP) and announced firing-origin hex. Output the occupied frame and front/side/rear facing derived from it; a completed ordinary position is handed to existing ordinary-facing Rules. CAFP-origin and same-hex queries are not evaluated, not treated as center shots. |

Every contract includes `inBypass`. Coordinates are individual Number facts, required to be integral in [-1000000, 1000000], with checked 64-bit intermediate arithmetic; an ordinary completed VCA is an integer 0-5 under `ScenarioA1Geometry.FacingDegrees`. Positions, modes, origin/completion kinds, end cause and stages are Choice facts; uncertain predicates are nullable Flag facts. The adapter validates enums, finite/integral coordinates and contradictory states before conversion; direct typed entry points enforce the same bounds and structural invariants. Conditional fields use `MayBeUnknown=true` and the calculator requires only the active branch. Missing public input is labelled missing, not represented as secret information. Invalid supplied facts remain invalid even if another branch would otherwise ignore them. Malformed/unsupported input is not-evaluated; a supported, known rule prohibition is refused. Not-applicable is never an allow verdict.

The existing `ContractVerdict` has outcomes/reasons/rolls, not arbitrary numeric output fields. Keep it unchanged: typed calculator verdicts hold positions, counts, costs and modifier components; contract adapters map these to declared outcomes and cited display reasons (including the values). Downstream production code calls the typed calculator, never parses reason text. Contract outcomes are `evaluated`, `refused`, `not-applicable`, `not-evaluated`, with `forced-end` additionally on the continuation contract. Named typed tests assert the numeric results; minigame checks assert outcomes and the Workbench inspection reads displayed values. Use the existing registry; do not add a generic output-schema feature to this batch.

Conditional-input acceptance is explicit. An actual shot-time turret turn does not need acquisition/repeated-target history. An unchanged front/rear ordinary-fire query does not need that history either. An unchanged-side query needs only the exception facts for its weapon category. Bounding First Fire needs `earlierMpTcaChange` as described in section 4. An `EndMovement` query needs no candidate destination or frame: for a voluntary end it uses pending/completed state, while known immobilization settles its forced-end disposition independently; missing geometry can still prevent the separate defensive-position query. A completed defensive query requires only its completed position and applicable firing-origin facts. Missing old frames must not block it. Tests cover each branch with irrelevant optional facts absent.

Each contract has an optional self-concerning Owner `vehicleId` Unit anchor. Private weapon profile, turret/shot history and acquisition applicability concern that anchor and are masked when it is absent or sealed. Announced position, movement/action, target occupied hex and hypothetical board geometry are All facts, explicitly labelled as announced/hypothetical; they are not authorization to inspect an enemy concealed counter. Public target geometry needs no enemy Unit identity. In the acquisition/history exceptions, gather only facts the firing side may know. A branch needing unavailable private inputs returns not-evaluated, not a guessed weapon profile or a diagnostic disclosing private history. Fully public movement/defensive-position branches may evaluate for either viewer without revealing an anchor identity. Unknown irrelevant history does not block an independently settled front/rear or actual-turn branch.

Movement stage and immobilization are independent: `TurnPending` plus immobilized retains the old occupied frame; `EntryCompleted` plus immobilized retains the completed position (ordinary or Bypass). Neither combination implies a further entry. The old frame is not required for an independently known completed position. Contradictory stage/position facts are rejected, not resolved by input order.

The contracts have no dice rolls or emitted game events. Places describe the firing adjustment, MPh VCA expenditure, continuation/end decision and Defensive First Fire window respectively. `asop_steps.py D2.321 D2.33` found zero exclusive ASOP steps; no shared phase row is claimed.

## 9. Delivery, records and performance
