# ASL D3.7 Vehicle Breakdown Claim Design

**Status:** Approved by the user and built, 2026-10-29 (section 10), after a sub-agent's review of the design (section 9). The D3.7 claim (Claude; the inventory's section 6, claimed on main as e330fe39, holds amended in ce67c504) takes D3.7 Malfunction and D3.71 Low Ammo B#, on the branch `claim/d3.7-breakdown`. The fifth rule claim since the last batch gate: E10.2's B12 benefit (`ScenarioA1AmmoVehicleCalculator.BreakdownNumber`, PR 66) needs D3.71's B# and X# before it can reach a shot. Rules only: no caller changes, so no recorded game replays differently. Rulings are numbered RD3.n; RD3.3, RD3.4, and RD3.5 carry readings the user may veto.

## 1. The rule (D3.7, D3.71, pp. 201 and 202; A.11, p. 42; A9.7, A9.71; A19.131, p. 86; A21.11, p. 88; E3.741; C5.62; G10.41, p. 335)

- **D3.7:** whenever a vehicle fires, that armament is subject to breakdown. A printed B# under 12 applies only to the MA unless Vehicle Notes say otherwise; numbers are printed only for armament with a B# under 12 or an X#, and B12 is assumed otherwise. Ordnance malfunctions on an Original To Hit DR at or above its B#, and an MG, FT, or IFE on an Original IFT or CC DR of 12. A Malfunction counter is placed, not a flip. Repair: once per RPh, a separate dr for each malfunctioned weapon, by any inherent crew not shocked or stunned [EXC: an AAMG needs a CE crew or a Hero Rider]: 1 repairs, 6 Disables (the counter flipped to its Disabled side), 2 to 5 nothing. A vehicle whose MA and all its Secondary Armament (if any, per its Vehicle Listing) are Disabled is Recalled (D5.341), unless it has Passenger or Towing capability.
- **D3.71:** "A Gun with a B#" (circled) malfunctions on an Original 12 To Hit or IFT DR and suffers Low Ammo on any other Original To Hit or IFT DR at or above the B#. Low Ammo covers every ammo type. The Low Ammo counter turns the original B# into an X# (A.11) and makes a new B# one lower. The IS-3 example: B11 circled; after Low Ammo, an Original 11 or 12 Disables the MA (and Recalls it), a 10 malfunctions it.
- **A.11:** a form of fire that lowers the B# also turns the Original B# into an X# for that use (B12 with Sustained Fire is B10/X12). C5.62 Intensive Fire lowers the B# only; A21.11 (captured) and E3.741 (Extreme Winter) lower "B#/X#", so an X# already there drops too. Reaching an X# removes a SW (A9.7); for a vehicle's weapon it is Disabled. **A19.131:** under an Ammunition Shortage every vehicle and Gun counts as having a circled B# one lower, and one that already has a circled B# starts with Low Ammo. **G10.41:** a further Low Ammo turns the current B# into the X#; an Ammunition Shortage counts as one occurrence.
- **What each side knows:** the Malfunction, Disabled, and Low Ammo counters are on the vehicle, and the DR is public; an unconcealed stack may be inspected (A12.16), and a vehicle that fires reveals itself.

## 3. Rulings

- **RD3.1 One calculator.** `ScenarioA1VehicleBreakdownCalculator`, with `ScenarioA1VehicleBreakdownModels.cs` and `ScenarioA1VehicleBreakdownContract.cs`. It decides one weapon's numbers and outcome; which of several firing MGs malfunctions stays A9.71's Random Selection in fire.
- **RD3.2 The numbers.** `Numbers(BreakdownNumberFacts)` gives `VehicleBreakdownNumbers(B#, X#)` for this use, in this order:
  1. The printed B# and printed X# (null: B12, no X#), applying to the MA only (a BMG, CMG, or AAMG, and an MG MA without its own number, take B12) unless a Vehicle Note extends them.
  2. A19.131: an Ammunition Shortage makes the MA's B# circled and one lower, or, if it is circled already, gives Low Ammo (a separate step, never an A.11 lowering).
  3. Low Ammo (D3.71, G10.41): the current B# becomes the X# and the B# one lower. So an IS-3 under Ammunition Shortage, circled B11, becomes circled B10 with Low Ammo: X10 and B9.
  4. A.11 lowerings: `lowersB` (C5.62) lowers the B# only; `lowersBX` (A21.11, E3.741, A19.32) lowers the B# and any X#. With no X# yet, a lowering makes the Original B# the X#, unlowered. So X11 and B10 after Low Ammo, captured (A21.11, 2), is X9 and B8; a B12 captured is B10 and X12.
- **RD3.3 The outcome.** `Outcome(originalDr, numbers, circled, lowAmmo, drKind)`, `drKind` To Hit, IFT, or CC: an X# reached Disables the weapon; else an Original 12 malfunctions it; else a circled B# before Low Ammo gives Low Ammo on a DR at or above the B# (the MA Gun's To Hit DR, or its IFT DR when it fires IFE or on the Area Target Type; an MG's only by a Vehicle Note); else a DR at or above the B# malfunctions it. Any weapon's numbers come from `Numbers`, so a lowered MG breaks down below 12. `VehicleBreakdownOutcome`: None, Malfunction, LowAmmo, Disabled. **A reading open to veto:** A19.131's circled B# reaches the MA only, as D3.7 limits a printed B#; the other reading gives every vehicle weapon one.
- **RD3.4 Repair.** `RepairRefusal(RepairFacts)` with `VehicleWeaponRepairBar`'s facts and texts: once per RPh for this weapon; a malfunctioned, not Disabled, weapon; for an MA, BMG, or CMG, an inherent crew not shocked or stunned, no Unconfirmed Kill, not Recalled (D5.341 reads it as Stun), not Abandoned; for an AAMG, a CE crew, or a Hero Rider, who replaces the crew test (**a reading open to veto**: D3.7's EXC names who may repair it, so the crew's state does not bar a Hero Rider). `RepairResult(dr)` returns the existing `RepairOutcome` (1 Repaired; 6 Eliminated, which for a vehicle weapon is its Disabled side; else none).
- **RD3.5 Recall.** `Recalled(maDisabled, everySecondaryDisabled, passengerOrTowing)`: the MA and every SA its Vehicle Listing gives it Disabled (an MG is SA only when listed in the SA column, D1.34; with none, the MA alone suffices), and no Passenger or Towing capability. Asked after a repair's 6 and after a fire result that Disables. An unknown SA state is not a Recall.
- **RD3.6 Old members.** Forwarding, answers equal: `VehicleMgRepairResult` (to `RepairResult`) and `VehicleWeaponRepairBar` (its body moved to `RepairRefusal` for an MA, BMG, or CMG). Keeping a different answer, each with its summary naming the calculator method and the difference: `ScenarioA1OrdnanceCalculator.Breakdown` and `Run`'s malfunction (no circled B#, X#, or Low Ammo; A19.131 not for vehicles); `ScenarioA1FireCalculator.VehicleWeaponEffect` (no X#); the AAMG path (`VehicleRepairPhaseBar`, `VehicleRepairWeaponBar`, `VehicleRepairCrewBarAsPlanned`, `VehicleRepairChange`) and `ScenarioA1RoutRallyProjection.VehicleRepairAllowedAsRecorded` (no shocked or stunned check, no Hero Rider; the generic `Disabled` for an AAMG); `VehicleWeaponRepairChanges` (Recall on the MA alone). Not touched, named in the wiring row: the inline tests in the canister, area, and armor calculators and `OverrunEffect`. `TurnPackingVulnerabilityRules.LowAmmoNumbers` is G10.41's and stays; its summary names `Numbers`. Changing a recorded fire's result (Low Ammo, Disabled) changes replays, so it is the wiring's, with older recorded games kept on their recorded reading.
- **RD3.7 The contract `d3.7-vehicle-breakdown`.** What does this Original DR do to this vehicle weapon? Facts, all disclosed to All (section 1; a concealed vehicle's facts are gathered as unknown for its opponent): `vehicleId` (Unit), `weapon` (MA, BMG, CMG, AAMG, SA), `printedB` and `printedX` (numbers, may be unknown), `circled`, `noteExtends`, `lowAmmo`, `ammunitionShortage`, `lowersB`, `lowersBX` (numbers), `drKind` (To Hit, IFT, CC), `originalDr`. Outcomes: `none`, `malfunction`, `low-ammo`, `disabled`, `not-evaluated`; the reason gives the B# and X#. Place: the fire phases. Events: `conditions-changed` (the Malfunction, Disabled, or Low Ammo counter).
