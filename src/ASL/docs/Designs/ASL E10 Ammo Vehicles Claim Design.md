# ASL E10 Ammo Vehicles Claim Design

**Status:** Approved by the user and built, 2026-10-29 (section 10), after a sub-agent's review of the design (section 9); RE10.6 and RE10.11 approved as proposed. The E10 claim (Claude; the inventory's section 6, claimed on main as c2d5b07b), built with the A24.5 claim as one task on the branch `claim/a24.5-e10` (the user, 2026-10-29; `ASL A24.5 Smoke Strength Claim Design.md`). It takes E10.1 from the proving slice, whose contract `e10.1-eligibility` only read the old calculator and keeps its answers. Rules only: no caller changes, so no recorded game replays differently. Rulings are numbered RE10.n; RE10.6 and RE10.11 are readings the user may veto.

## 1. The rule (E10, p. 244; D.7, p. 192; D3.71, pp. 201 and 202; C8.9, p. 179; A12.16; ASOP 8.43B, p. 681; Vehicle Note 93, p. 348; H1.49, p. 332; H1.6)

- **E10.1, E10.11:** a SSR may give a vehicle with a B# an Ammo Vehicle. Unless the SSR or its Vehicle Note names one, any vehicle with at least 15 PP of Passenger capacity serves (at least 20 PP when the armament with the B# is 100mm or more). The DYO cost is H1.49's.
- **E10.12:** an Ammo Supply counter marks the Ammo Vehicle, reduces its usable Passenger capacity to zero, and may not be removed unless the vehicle is Immobile (E10.4) or the supply Depleted (E10.3).
- **E10.2:** when the correct Ammo Vehicle is in the same or an Accessible Location to the proper CE armed vehicle when it fires, its B# is treated as a normal B12. One Ammo Vehicle supplies at most two identically armed vehicles (Gun caliber and barrel length) at a time.
- **E10.21:** once the benefit is used for Prep Fire, the Ammo Vehicle may not spend MP in the next MPh (marked with a Prep Fire counter). The benefit is not used in Bounding (First) Fire; it may be in the AFPh.
- **E10.3:** a vehicle with a B# that suffers Low Ammo (D3.71) is Replenished after it and its Ammo Vehicle have been TI in the same or Accessible Locations for complete Game Turns (RPh to CCPh inclusive) by its B# (B9: 1, B10: 2, B11: 3). The armed vehicle stays CE. The TI ends at completion, or if the crew loses CE, either vehicle spends MP or fires, or either is attacked in CC; an interrupted Game Turn does not count, and the armed vehicle then fires that weapon on its B#. One Ammo Vehicle Replenishes up to two identically armed vehicles at once. After each completed Replenishment the Ammo Vehicle makes an Ammunition DR; an Original 12 exhausts it, the Ammo Supply counter is removed, and the vehicle takes its normal portage again. ASOP 8.43B keeps this TI at the end of the CCPh, where completion is checked.
- **E10.31:** every Ammo Type depleted by normal Depletion (C8.9) is Replenished, but only B# vehicles with Low Ammo can be Replenished.
- **E10.4:** an unarmored Ammo Vehicle that becomes Immobilized is not flipped to its Wreck side: it is marked Immobilized and keeps supplying. An Ammo Vehicle may offload to form an Ammo Dump in its Location, or an Immobile one may transfer its ammunition to another Accessible or same-Location vehicle that qualifies under E10.11, by the Replenishment procedure for four Game Turns; at the end of the CCPh the counter moves, and an Immobilized original is flipped to its Wreck side. A vehicle may not spend MP in the turn it becomes, or ceases to be, an Ammo Vehicle.
- **E10.5:** an Ammo Vehicle that becomes a Burning Wreck (or would) explodes at once, removing its wreck, as a detonated Goliath with no designated target and no X# (Vehicle Note 93: one Effects DR for every attack, the charge at the vehicle's level; 36 FP vs unarmored units in its hex and 16 FP in each adjacent hex; an AFV in its hex attacked as Successfully Positioned against its weakest rear AF, never Optimal, and an adjacent AFV as Poorly Positioned; Fortifications only in its hex).
- **E10.6:** a SSR or a DYO purchase (H1.6) may create an immobile Ammo Dump; every Ammo Vehicle rule applies to it as a stationary Location.
- **What each side knows:** a Low Ammo vehicle is marked with a Low Ammo counter (D3.71), Depletion follows an announced use and a public To Hit DR (C8.9), and the Ammo Supply counter is on its vehicle; any stack not concealed may be inspected (A12.16). So these are known to both sides, unless under a "?".

## 2. What decides it today

Inside Rules (the claim's to merge):
- `TurnAmmoEligibilityRules` **decides** selection (`SelectVehicle`, :33), Immobile by D.7 (`Immobile`, :55), same or Accessible (`SameOrAccessible`, :64), identical armament (`Compatible`, :71), and a three-valued recipient set (`EvaluateRecipients`, :81: Eligible, Ineligible, NotEvaluated; a complete inventory of recipients required; the two-vehicle limit; the Bounding Fire bar; no TI check). These match the PDF. Only `e10.1-eligibility` (`ScenarioA1RuleContracts.cs:284`) calls them; nothing in fire does.
- `TurnWorkRules.Evaluate` (`TurnWorkRules.cs:31`) **decides** the Replenishment duration from the circled B# before Low Ammo (`originalBNumber`: 1 to 9 one turn, B10 2, B11 3, otherwise none) and the transfer's 4; `Interrupted` (:70) the interruptions. Shared with G10 Animal Packing.
- `TurnWorkEffects` **decides** the zero Passenger capacity (`PassengerCapacity(ordinary, hasSupply)`, :98), Replenishment's CE posture (`ReplenishmentPostureSupported`, :68), its place and load (`ReplenishmentReady`, :74: same Location only, where E10.3 allows an Accessible one, and no Passengers aboard the supply), whether supply may move (`CanRelocateSupply`, :65, Immobile read as Immobilized or Bogged only), the transfer source (`RelocationSourceReady`, :91: Immobile, and also no occupants and no held equipment, an engine simplification E10 does not state) and destination (`TransferDestinationReady`, :95: same Location only, no E10.11 check), the wreck after a transfer (`WreckAfterRelocation`, :66), and that a completed Replenishment restores ammunition (`Resolve`).
- `TurnWorkLifecycle` **decides** that a completed Replenishment needs an Ammunition DR (`RequiresAmmunitionDr`) and that an Original 12 exhausts the supply (`Change`, :120).
- Missing: E10.21's MP bar; E10.4's unarmored Immobilized Ammo Vehicle and its MP bar; E10.5; E10.6 as a supply source; what E10.31 restores (Units decides it).

Outside Rules (track 3's, the wiring row):
- `Units/State/GameProjector.WorkEffects.cs`: `PrepareWorkEffects` (:9) decides one available supply per vehicle (:21); the CE posture (:60) applies only to newer records; (:69) reads "co-located" as one Location; Replenishment never checks `Compatible` or TI (:56 to :75); `ValidateSupplyRelocation` (:99) reads Immobile as Immobilized or Bogged, (:108) decides a dump is in the same Location, (:116) decides the destination is in the same Location with no supply; `CompleteWorkEffects` (:153) decides every depleted Ammo Type is restored.
- `Units/State/GameProjector.TurnWork.cs:36` caps a participant at one open work, so an Ammo Vehicle at one recipient where E10.3 allows two.
- `Play/GamePlanner.Passengers.cs:27` asks `PassengerCapacity`; `Play/GamePlanner.TurnWork.cs:51` asks `RequiresAmmunitionDr`; `ASL.Turns/TurnWorkProgress.cs:84` asks `Evaluate` and (:100) repeats the durations in its text.
- No fire code applies the E10.2 benefit: `ScenarioA1OrdnanceCalculator.Breakdown` uses the printed B#, and D3.71 (Low Ammo, the B# becoming an X#) is not built; its row is D3.71's (pass 105).

## 3. Rulings

- **RE10.1 One calculator.** `ScenarioA1AmmoVehicleCalculator`, with `ScenarioA1AmmoVehicleModels.cs` and `ScenarioA1AmmoVehicleContract.cs`. The `TurnAmmo*` records and enums move to the models file unchanged (same names, same namespace). Each `TurnAmmoEligibilityRules` member becomes a one-line call into a calculator member that holds its body: `Selection`, `Immobile`, `SameOrAccessible`, `Compatible`, and `Recipients` (today's three-valued `EvaluateRecipients`, unchanged). The new refusals are built on top of these.
- **RE10.2 Selection and portage (E10.11, E10.12).** `Selection(...)` as `SelectVehicle`. `PassengerCapacity(ordinary, hasSupply)` gives zero with supply. `SupplyRemovalRefusal(immobile, depleted)` refuses removing the counter unless Immobile or Depleted. **Immobile is D.7's one definition** (`Immobile`: Bogged, Mired, Immobilized, Abandoned, inherent crew broken, Shocked, Stunned); a dump is always Immobile.
- **RE10.3 The benefit (E10.2, E10.21).** "The correct Ammo Vehicle" is the supply authorized for this vehicle (by SSR), of its side, and identically armed (`Compatible`). The vehicles it "serves now" are the ones whose benefit it gave this Player Turn and that are still in its same or an Accessible Location. `BenefitRefusal(BenefitFacts)`: the correct Ammo Vehicle, the firer CE, same or Accessible, at most two served including this one, not Bounding (First) Fire; the AFPh allowed. `BenefitBarsMovement(phase)`: in the PFPh the Ammo Vehicle takes a Prep Fire counter and spends no MP in the next MPh.
- **RE10.4 Replenishment (E10.3).** `ReplenishmentTurns(originalBNumber)` reads the circled B# before Low Ammo, as `TurnWorkRules.Evaluate` does: 1 to 9 one Game Turn (the table prints 9; a lower circled B#, as under A19.131, needs no more time), B10 2, B11 3, none for 0 or less and 12 or more. `TransferTurns` gives 4. `ReplenishmentRefusal(ReplenishmentFacts)`: the recipient has a B# and Low Ammo (E10.31), is CE, and is in the same or an Accessible Location; both are TI (a dump trivially); they are identically armed; no Passenger is aboard the supply (E10.12's zero capacity); and the supply Replenishes at most two at once. A complete Game Turn runs RPh to CCPh; completion is checked at the end of the CCPh (ASOP 8.43B). `Interrupted` stays in `TurnWorkRules` (shared, generic). `SupplyExhausted(originalDr)` is an Original 12, after which the vehicle takes its normal portage again.
- **RE10.5 What is restored (E10.31).** `Restored(lowAmmo, depletedTypes)` gives the Low Ammo ended and every C8.9-depleted Ammo Type restored, only for a Low Ammo vehicle; nothing otherwise. An interrupted Replenishment restores nothing, and the vehicle fires on its B#.
- **RE10.6 Immobilization and transfer (E10.4).** `WreckWhenImmobilized(armored, ammoVehicle)` is false for an unarmored Ammo Vehicle. `TransferRefusal(TransferFacts)`: the destination is the same or an Accessible Location's vehicle that qualifies under E10.11 and holds no supply, or a dump in the source's own Location. **The source must be Immobile or its supply Depleted, for an offload too** (a reading open to veto): E10.12 bars removing the counter otherwise, and E10.4's "can offload" names the procedure, not an exception. The other reading lets any Ammo Vehicle offload. Today's code already requires Immobile, so this reading changes nothing in play. Both go by the four-Game-Turn procedure. `WreckAfterTransfer(immobilized)` flips the Immobilized original. `MovementBarred(becameOrCeasedThisTurn)` bars MP in that turn.
- **RE10.7 The burning Ammo Vehicle (E10.5).** `Explosion(ammoVehicle, burningOrWouldBe)` gives, when it explodes, the wreck removed and Vehicle Note 93's attacks as facts for the Placed DC's calculator (A23): one Effects DR, the charge's level, 36 FP vs unarmored units in its hex and 16 FP in each adjacent hex, an AFV in its hex Successfully Positioned vs its weakest rear AF (never Optimal), an adjacent AFV Poorly Positioned, Fortifications only in its hex, no designated target and no X#.
- **RE10.8 The Ammo Dump (E10.6).** The supply's facts carry `IsDump`: always Immobile, no Passenger capacity, no wreck, TI trivially, and a source in its own Location to every rule above. Creating one is a card's SSR or a DYO purchase: the wiring row's.
- **RE10.9 Contracts.** `e10.1-eligibility` stays as it is (`ScenarioA1RuleContracts.cs` is the C9.3 claim's held file) and answers as before. Two new contracts in `ScenarioA1AmmoVehicleContract.cs`: `e10.2-benefit` (does this shot fire on a B12? subjects `supplyId` and `firerId`; outcomes `b12`, `printed-b`, `not-evaluated`) and `e10.3-replenishment` (may this Replenishment start? subjects `supplyId` and `recipientId`; outcomes `may-start`, `refused`, `not-evaluated`). Their facts are disclosed to All, the Ammo Supply, Low Ammo, and depletion included (section 1); a concealed vehicle's facts are gathered as unknown for its opponent, so the verdict is then not evaluated.
- **RE10.10 Old members.** Forwarding, answers equal: every `TurnAmmoEligibilityRules` member, `TurnWorkEffects.PassengerCapacity`, `WreckAfterRelocation`, `ReplenishmentPostureSupported`, `TurnWorkLifecycle.RequiresAmmunitionDr`, the Original 12 in `TurnWorkLifecycle.Change`, and the E10 durations in `TurnWorkRules.Evaluate`. Keeping a different answer, each with a summary naming the calculator method and the difference: `CanRelocateSupply` (Immobile by Immobilized or Bogged only), `ReplenishmentReady` (same Location only; its Passenger check is kept in `ReplenishmentRefusal`), `RelocationSourceReady` (no occupants and no held equipment, which E10 does not ask), `TransferDestinationReady` (same Location only, no E10.11), and `Resolve`'s restoration (all depleted ammunition, with no Low Ammo condition). G10's members are untouched.
- **RE10.11 The benefit under Low Ammo** (a reading open to veto). After Low Ammo the circled B# becomes an X# and a new B# one lower applies (D3.71). `BreakdownNumber(printedB, xNumber, benefit)` gives, with the benefit, a normal B12 and no X#: "treated as a normal B12" means the gun breaks down as an uncircled B12, with none of the circled B#'s Low Ammo effects. The other reading keeps the X#. Without the benefit it returns the B# and X# as given.
