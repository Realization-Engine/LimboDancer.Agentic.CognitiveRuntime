A follow-up of the C9.3 claim (Claude; built), on its branch `claim/c9.3-spotters`, in the files it holds (`ScenarioA1SpotterCalculator.cs`, `ScenarioA1SpotterModels.cs`, `ScenarioA1SpotterContract.cs`). ## 1. The rule (C9.3, p. 179; ASOP 3.11A, p. 679; A.7, p. 43; A4.8, p. 52)

- **C9.3:** a new Spotter may not be designated until the original is eliminated, broken, or captured, "and not until the start of the owner's MPh following such a loss", except that a Reduced Spotting squad's surviving Good Order HS keeps spotting. Spotting "is considered the equivalent of using a SW for purposes of movement curtailment and inherent FP loss"; the original Spotter is designated in the owner's PFPh or DFPh and marked with that phase's fire counter "for having used a SW during that phase".
- **ASOP (p. 678):** it lists "the official order of actions", taken in order. **3.1, START OF THE MPh:** 3.11A "May designate new mortar Spotter for one eliminated or not in Good Order (C9.3)", then 3.12D (the DEFENDER's Starshells), then 3.13A (Gliders). **3.2, START OF ITS MPh:** 3.21A (a berserk unit's charge), then 3.22A (any other unit's move). ASOP 2.22A and 4.21D give the PFPh and DFPh designation only to a mortar "that had no original Spotter".
- **A.7:** Good Order is not "broken, berserk, captured, stunned, shocked, or held in Melee". **A4.8:** a TI unit "may not move, advance, fire" during its Player Turn, and its TI counter is removed at the end of its CCPh.

## 2. What exists and what is missing

- `ScenarioA1SpotterCalculator.Timing` returns `ReplacementWindow` for the owner's MPh after a recorded elimination, break, or capture, but only when the caller says the position is the MPh's opening (`TurnCheckpoint.MovementStart`); nothing in Rules says when that opening has passed. Its comparison of loss and now is sound: the projector's `PlayerTurnOrdinal` counts across the whole game.
- `DesignationRefusal` admits an original designation only, and `FireCounter` throws outside the PFPh and DFPh. Both keep their answers.
- `CandidateRefusal`, `InReach`, `SharesLocation`, `Retains`, and `HoldsAssignment` serve a replacement unchanged.
- **Found by the review:** today only the original designation marks the Spotter (`GameProjector.SpotterDesignation.cs`, the fire counter and `NoMoveThisPlayerTurn`). The fire that spots (`GameProjector.cs`, the `ordnance-fired` projection near line 1478) records the SW use and the Spotter's use but places no fire counter and no movement bar. So a Spotter that spots in a later phase than its designation, a retained original today or a replacement once wired, keeps its movement, against C9.3's SW equivalence.

## 3. Rulings

- **RC93.11 The opening of the MPh.** A new enum `MovementOpeningStep`: `SpotterReplacement` (3.11A), `Starshell` (3.12D), `GliderPlacement` (3.13A), `BerserkCharge` (3.21A), `UnitMovement` (3.22A), and `Other`. `ScenarioA1SpotterCalculator.OpeningOpen(IReadOnlyList<MovementOpeningStep> taken)`: the opening is open while every step taken in this MPh is a 3.11A replacement, so several mortars' Spotters may be replaced in turn; any other step closes it for the rest of the phase, even after that move ends. `OpeningCheckpoint(taken)` gives `TurnCheckpoint.MovementStart` while it is open and `UnitMovement` after, the checkpoint the caller hands to `Timing`. **A step is one recorded command of the MPh**, after the batch that changed the phase; the events the phase change writes itself are not a step, and a refused, stale, or cancelled proposal writes nothing and is not one. Each command maps to a step by the events it recorded: `mortar-spotter-designated` alone (with its dice and conditions) is `SpotterReplacement`; `starshell-fired` is `Starshell`; a command with `movement-step`, `vehicle-step`, `instance-moved`, `movement-ended`, `motion-attempted`, `entry-attempted`, `overrun-declared`, `gun-hooked`, `manhandling-rolled`, `units-searched`, or `turn-work-recorded` is `UnitMovement` (a berserk charge among them; both close the opening alike); Gliders are not built; any other command is `Other`, which closes it: an action Rules does not know never keeps the window open. The calculator holds this table as `OpeningStepOf(IReadOnlyCollection<string> eventTypes)`, so the caller only hands over each command's event types.
- **RC93.12 The replacement's verdict.** `SpotterReplacementFacts`: the timing (`SpotterTimingFacts`, whose `Now` is the owner's MPh); the steps taken this MPh; whether the weapon is an active mortar whose crew is on the map; the candidate (`SpotterCandidateFacts`); the hex range; the sharing fact; night; and a decision or move still open. `Replacement(facts)` checks, in this order: a pending decision; not an active mortar; the candidate's refusal (`CandidateRefusal`, which bars a Melee, Captured, or broken unit and the mortar's own crew); night (its own reason: E1.89 and E1.91, spotting at night, are not built); the opening closed (RC93.11); the timing. A timing of `NotEvaluated` gives the verdict "not evaluated", not a refusal; any other timing but `ReplacementWindow` refuses with `Timing`'s reason. Then the range or the sharing unknown (not evaluated); out of reach (`InReach`); not sharing (`SharesLocation`). **No TI check** (the review's finding 2): a replacement uses nothing, and the new Spotter's first spotted fire is in the opponent's DFPh (`SpottedFireAllowed`), after its TI counter is removed; the original designation keeps its TI refusals, since it is a use in that phase.
- **RC93.13 What a replacement does, and what spotting does.** `SpotterReplacementVerdict(State, Refusal, EstablishesAssignment, Reason)`, State one of may-replace, refused, not-evaluated: when allowed, the new Spotter holds the mortar's assignment from now on (a new assignment; the lost one stays lost), with **no fire counter and no movement bar**: C9.3's counter belongs to the PFPh or DFPh designation, where the Spotter is used as a SW in that phase, and a replacement at the MPh's opening is a designation, not a use. **Spotting is the use:** `SpottingMarks(phase, alreadyMarkedThisPhase)` says that the fire that spots marks its Spotter with `FireCounter(phase)` and, in the PFPh, `BarsMovement`, unless the Spotter already carries that phase's counter (its designation this phase, or an earlier spotted shot). Today's projector does not ask it; that site and its behavior change go into the W-C9.3 wiring row on main (Codex holds the projector), not the backlog.
- **RC93.14 One assignment at a time, and the same unit again.** A replacement is refused while the mortar's assignment is not lost (`Timing` gives `Retained`), so a second replacement of the same mortar at the same opening is refused. A rallied original Spotter may be designated again (RC93.6). Every `recordedLoss` fact the calculator takes (`Retains`, `HoldsAssignment`, `FireBar`) means a loss of the current assignment, never of an earlier one with the same pair of units; the summaries say so, and the wiring gathers it so (W-C9.3, section 3, item 2).
- **RC93.15 What the loss is.** C9.3's three losses: eliminated, broken, captured (`SpotterChange`). ASOP 3.11A's "not in Good Order" is wider by A.7 (berserk, stunned, shocked, held in Melee). Until backlog 80's berserk and Melee row is built, a berserk or Melee Spotter's mortar has neither spotted fire (`Retains` and `CandidateRefusal` refuse it) nor a replacement; that row is unchanged.
- **RC93.16 The contract `c9.3-spotter-replacement`.** May this unit be designated the mortar's new Spotter now? Two subjects, each a unit the map offers: `holderId`, the unit that holds the mortar (a mortar is equipment, which the map does not offer; the C9 claim's lesson), and `spotterId`, the candidate. The mortar's facts, `hasDesignation`, `lossCause` (eliminated, broken, captured, reduced, none, unknown), `reducedSurvivorGoodOrder`, `lossBeforeThisPhase` (recorded before this MPh began), and `openingClosed` (a step other than a replacement has been taken this MPh) are the owner's of `holderId`, so a viewer who picks its own unit as the candidate for the enemy's mortar learns nothing of the enemy's loss (the review's finding 3). The candidate's facts are the owner's of `spotterId`. Outcomes: may-replace, refused, not-evaluated; `lossCause` unknown gives not-evaluated. Events: `mortar-spotter-designated` (the new assignment; no fire counter). Place: MPh, ASOP 3.11A. The contract builds the positions `Timing` reads from these flags; its numbers are read only as whole numbers an int holds.
- **RC93.17 Old members.** `DesignationRefusal`, `FireCounter`, `BarsMovement`, and `Timing` keep their callers and answers. Nothing forwards, since no old member decides a replacement or a spotted shot's marks.

## 5. Tests and minigame

- `ScenarioA1SpotterReplacementTests`: the opening (only replacements keep it open; each other step and `Other` close it; the empty list is open; `OpeningStepOf` for each named event type and an unknown one); each refusal in order; not evaluated for an unknown timing, range, or sharing; the window after each loss and not after a reduction with a Good Order survivor or with no loss; refused in the opponent's MPh, in the PFPh, and in the MPh of the loss; the effects; the same unit again; a second replacement of the same mortar refused; `SpottingMarks` in the PFPh, the DFPh, and when already marked.
- The contract: its verdicts; the opponent's view of the mortar's facts not evaluated; a viewer's own candidate for the enemy's mortar learns no loss fact.
- The `c9-spotter` bundle gains the replacement contract and checks: a broken Spotter replaced at the next MPh's opening; refused once a unit has moved; refused in the MPh of the loss; a reduced squad's Good Order HS needing none; the opponent's view not evaluated.
