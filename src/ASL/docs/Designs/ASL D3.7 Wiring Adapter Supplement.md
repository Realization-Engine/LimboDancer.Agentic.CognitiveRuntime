## Purpose and boundaries

The original calculator gives a weapon's numbers and outcome, but the legacy resolvers still interpret a threshold as Malfunction. Add opt-in resolution overloads using supplemental raw facts without adding any field to frozen `OrdnanceShot` or `FireAttack`. Every old signature delegates to the old reading. New optional result properties are omitted from JSON for legacy calls.

`VehicleBreakdownUseFacts` carries actual weapon identity category, printed B/X, circled/extension status, existing Low Ammo, shortage, captured and non-qualified use, the recorded winter reduction and Intensive Fire. `ScenarioA1VehicleBreakdownResolutionCalculator` derives lowerings and the appropriate DR kind in Rules. Its result contains the calculated numbers and one of None, Malfunction, LowAmmo or Disabled. `Failed` distinguishes Malfunction/Disabled from Low Ammo. The new entry points accept only the corresponding actual vehicle weapon and reject missing or mismatched evidence; they cannot turn a SW into vehicle armament.

The D3.7/D3.71 reading follows physical PDF pp. 201 and 202, A.11 p. 43, A19.131 p. 86, A21.11/.13 p. 88, C5.62 p. 173, E3.741 p. 231 and D7.17 p. 207. A19.131 excludes CC, not an OVR's IFT use. Mounted vehicle weapons do not inherit an Infantry SW's inexperience or Sustained Fire facts. Preserve the existing distinction between current attack resolution and the failed weapon's subsequent ROF/acquisition and depletion exception; a malfunction does not retroactively erase the current attack.

## Resolution sites

- Ordnance `Resolve` and the ordnance, armor, canister and area `Run` paths receive the supplemental facts explicitly. No ambient state or thread-static context. The outcome is available before ROF, acquisition and ammunition-depletion decisions. Results expose `VehicleBreakdown` only for the new reading.
- A Gun's qualifying Area IFT roll is the shared attack roll, not an individual target's morale/collateral check. The composition reading for approved RD3.3 is that both qualifying rolls of one use read the pre-shot numbers; newly gained Low Ammo changes later uses. This timing is an explicit composition reading, not a direct quotation from the PDF. Retain the strongest weapon outcome and independently preserve `LowAmmoGained`, so a later malfunction never erases the Low Ammo counter gained on the other roll. The adapter does not admit an unsupported vehicle/target mode into public shot authorization.
- Fire's opt-in context identifies each actually firing vehicle weapon. A9.71 selection remains in the fire resolver; D3.7 gives the selected weapon's outcome. OVR retains D7.17's existing unsupported-target boundaries and weaponless immobilization behavior. A new adapter does not extend target eligibility.
- Fire follow-ups translate the new structured result into the existing weapon-specific condition vocabulary. Low Ammo is a structured effect for the caller's existing state collection, not a fabricated condition string. Recall is separately asked of the existing calculator with all listed SA states.

C8.9, physical PDF p. 179, explicitly includes both Gun Malfunction and Low Ammo as exceptions to the otherwise-unused depleted-ammunition attempt. The adapter therefore separates `CountsAsFiredOnDepletionFailure` from `Failed`: Low Ammo records a firing attempt and its state effect without barring ROF/acquisition as a weapon failure would. The old null-supplement path retains its old result. Cross-review caught and corrected the initial implementation's omission before caller wiring or test acceptance.

A supplied Hero must be active, friendly and aboard this vehicle as a Rider; an old Boolean alone is not evidence. The new refusal path applies the existing D3.7 repair requirements to SA and uses the actual per-weapon attempt. Existing repair wrappers retain their strings and results.

The new Workbench contract `d3.7-vehicle-breakdown-use` has an owner-scoped vehicle fact and owner-scoped dependent facts. A required `numbersKnown` fact distinguishes an absent printed number from inaccessible information: false or unknown is not evaluated, never assumed B12. No automatic Play fact source is added in this Rules claim. A12.16, p. 79, restricts unconcealed-but-uninspectable enemies too. Correct the original contract's A.11 citation and incomplete explanatory summary without changing the original contract's field shape or result behavior.

## Review by Claude, 2026-10-30

Claude, the D3.7 claim's reviewer, reviewed PR 71 as merged (7ccfc8e5, `git diff 7ccfc8e5^1 7ccfc8e5`), read-only, against the PDF (D3.7, D3.71 pp. 201 and 202, A.11 p. 43, C8.9 p. 179, D7.17 p. 207). Verdict: reviewed, no blocking finding; the claim is set to "reviewed". Counts: P1 0, P2 2, P3 4.

- **Held:** the outcomes follow D3.7 and D3.71, and the bundle reproduces the IS-3 example (p. 202). Every changed member keeps a forwarding legacy overload with null facts, so no recorded answer changes; the new result fields are nullable and omitted when null. The 20 adapter cases and 7 new bundle cases prove both readings.
- **P2 1, open:** a circled B# gives Low Ammo on an Overrun and on Canister. RD3.3 lists only a Gun's IFT DR when it fires IFE or on the Area Target Type, and D7.17 (p. 207) says a weapon with a B# or X# below 12 "would malfunction" on an Original OVR DR at or above it. Fix: a ruling open to the user's veto for the OVR and Canister readings, or map the Overrun so a circled B# malfunctions, with a test. To settle before wire-fire-3 calls these adapters.
- **P2 2, open:** the supplied printed B# and X# are never checked against the catalog (the ordnance and fire conflict checks compare Intensive Fire, winter, shortage, and the captured count only); a test resolves B12 facts against a B11 Gun. The supplement says mismatched evidence is refused. Fix: refuse with `vehicle-breakdown-facts-conflict` when the printed B# differs from the definition's (the E10.2 benefit by a named fact), or state that the facts are authoritative and name the catalog source in W-D3.7.
- **P3, open:** the captured-vehicle Ammunition Shortage reading (A21.11 p. 88 against A19.131 p. 86) and the Area pre-shot reading carry no ruling number; the two contracts cite D3.71 on different pages (cite 201 in both); the original contract's summary says hidden facts stay unknown while its facts are declared visible to All, and its backlog row was not updated; `RepairRefusal` builds the SA text with `Replace("MA", ...)`, which also rewrites a vehicle id containing "MA".
