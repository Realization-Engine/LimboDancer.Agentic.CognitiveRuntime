# ASL Unit Backlog Pass 25 Design

**Status:** Built. Pass 25 (Entry and exit) of the [ASL Card Play and Map Studio Redesign Plan](<ASL Card Play and Map Studio Redesign Plan.md>), section 5, the third game pass.

**Date:** 2026-10-01

**Related documents:** the [Scenario A1 Backlog Pass 25 Review](<Scenario A1 Backlog Pass 25 Review 2026-10-01.md>), the [ASL Unit Backlog Pass 20 Design](<ASL Unit Backlog Pass 20 Design.md>) (reinforcements entering), the [ASL Unit Backlog Pass 21 Design](<ASL Unit Backlog Pass 21 Design.md>) (Infantry leaving the map, Exit VP), the [ASL Unit Backlog Pass 24 Design](<ASL Unit Backlog Pass 24 Design.md>) (CVP of captured units), and the [ASL Unit Backlog](<ASL Unit Backlog.md>), section 39.

Rulings R25.1 to R25.7 are in the [ASL Unit Backlog Passes Plan](<ASL Unit Backlog Passes Plan.md>), section 5. Rule citations give physical pages of the registered rulebook PDF, `eASLRB_v3_01.pdf` (SHA-256 `957de75b...a247`).

## 1. Outcome

- **Entry by advance (task 25.1; R25.1).** `PlanAdvanceUnits` takes a stack waiting off board: `EntryCheck` (with the advance flag) checks its entry turn, one edge, a ground-level hex of the edge open to it this Game Turn, and the playable area; the cost is `EntryGround`, the hex's own terrain across the edge hexside; A4.72's CX and every other advance rule follow, a hex of enemy units included. The phase hold moved from the MPh to the APh: `EntryDue` holds the APh while a due unit waits and a hex open to it holds no Known enemy unit (`EntryHexBar`). The projector's advance already placed an off-map unit, so `advanced` needed no change for entry.
- **Blocked and delayed entry (task 25.2; R25.2).** A card's entry area may name `hexes` on its `board`; validation accepts well-formed hexes on a board of the card. `EntryFor` now returns the entry area with its turn and edge, and `EntryHexesFor` gives the hexes open in this Game Turn: the whole edge for an edge entry; the named hexes on the entry turn; then the edge hexes within four hexes of a named hex per Game Turn of delay, never past a hex of Water, River, or Canal terrain on the edge (`IsRiver`, by hex distance through the river). `EntryHexBar` obstructs a hex with a Known enemy unit or an enemy vehicle, or terrain whose cost is not decided; concealed units, Residual FP, and Fire Lanes no longer obstruct it.
- **Entry through the full step (task 25.3; R25.3).** `PlanEnter` is gone. `PlanMove` takes an entering stack through its own step: after `EntryCheck`, `EntryStep` costs the entry as `GroundStep` (split out of `InfantryStep`) across the edge hexside at the hex's own level, or as `BypassStep` (split out of `MoveEntry`) along hexsides starting at a vertex of the edge hexside; then Minimum Move, the leader bonus, Residual FP, Fire Lanes, and A12.15 run as at any step. A stack forced back off board is a `movement-step` whose `Attempted` equals its `To` with movers off map: the projector spends the MF, ends their move, and leaves no moving stack; the gate reads that back. `BypassEntered` gives a stack that entered in Bypass its entering hexside from the edge, so it can leave its Bypass or occupy the obstacle. SMOKE, a DC, and a pushed Gun from off board are refused (A2.52).
- **Offboard actions (task 25.4; R25.4).** `PlanDeploy` accepts a squad waiting off board from its entry turn's RPh, with a leader waiting along the same edge (`DirectingLeader` with no Location); the HS keep the squad's group and off-board position. `OffMapPosition.Entry` (event field `position.entry`) names the entry area of an off-board counter; `ScenarioSetup` refuses one its group lacks and matches lines by it. The setup planner refuses a unit naming another side's OB group (`play.setup-group`).
- **Exits (task 25.5; R25.5).** `PlanExit` serves the MPh and, through `asl.game.advance` naming an edge, the APh (`AdvanceMoved.Exit`, event field `exit`). The cost is the cheapest edge hexside's `EntryGround` (road rate across a road hexside); from Bypass, one MF when the far vertex of the last Bypass hexside is on the edge. A Guard leaves only off its side's Friendly Board Edge or an exit condition's edge. The projector's `ExitUnits` exits the movers, their prisoners, and the SW they hold; `UnitExit` gains `CapturedBy` (the prisoner's captor) and `Escort` (a Guard with prisoners). `ScenarioVictory` counts an exited prisoner as captured for its captor's CVP (double at the end) and, off the captor's exit condition, for its Exit VP (double at the end); an escorting Guard is not eliminated for CVP.
- **Components (task 25.6; R25.6).** Thirteen components under `Components/Play`, each holding no draft: S12 `UnitSelectionList`, S11 `LocationField`, A11 `MovementStatus`, A13 `InfantryMovementAction` (its options as one `MoveDraft` record), K09 `MapExitAction` (in the move toolbar and in the advance panel), A14 `SmokeGrenadeAction`, N03 `PlaceDemolitionChargeAction`, N04 `BerserkRetainedWeaponsField`, A15 `MovementWindowActions`, A16 `ReactionFireAction`, A23 `AdvanceActionPanel`, A01 `BuildingEntryAction`, and C19 `OpenEntryDeclaration`. The page keeps the drafts and the reset helpers of section 15.3 and computes every list from the view (`SeenOnly`, `VisibleUnits`). The page now offers off-board units in the advance panel with their edge hexes as destinations (in their order along the board), an advance's exit, the off-board squads and leaders for Deployment, and notes naming the units that wait off board. K08 shows its Location note only with a Location row.
- **Messages and small fixes (task 25.7; R25.7).** The SMOKE choices (`GamePlanner.SmokeExponent` made public); the pass that ends a move with no member left and the plainer end-move refusal (the projector's `EndMovement` no longer fails on an eliminated mover); the setup group refusal; the offboard Deployment refusal; the exit refusal naming the edge hexes beside it. The visual check added: TI units are not offered as movers.

- **After the reviews.** `AdvanceEntries` tests each waiting unit as the advance would (open hex, no marsh, a decided edge hexside, not pinned, MF after portage, no CX into Difficult Terrain), so the APh hold cannot deadlock, and `play.entry-due` names every waiting unit and open hexes; `OpenEntryHexes` gives the page the hexes a named entry may use in the MPh. `EntryCheck` refuses an entering stack beyond the stacking limits and a named entry with no hex on its edge. `AdvanceAid` (the leader's MF and IPC) serves the advance and the exit by advance. A Guard may leave by any edge; `ScenarioVictory.EscortEdge` decides whether it is spared from CVP. `ExitScoring` ends each exit's text with what it counts for. On the page: the CC Reaction Fire panel is the DEFENDER's and the adjudicator's only and reads the view, the Deploy lists a side's own squads (off-board ones marked) and seen leaders, the SMOKE, DC, and advance drafts reset and are pruned (`PruneMoveDrafts`), SMOKE and DC are not offered off board, the move and advance checkboxes are fieldsets (`Legend`), selects and buttons gain `aria-describedby` and ready notes, the exit edges read with their compass direction, and notes name the open named entry hexes and the stacks forced back.

## 2. Event format

| Where | Added | Read when missing |
|---|---|---|
| a position, `offMap: true` | `entry`: the entry area's id | The line's entry, else the group's first |
| `advanced` | `exit`: the edge the units leave by from their own Location | An advance on the map |
| `movement-step` | `attempted` equal to `to`, with movers off map: forced back off board | (no change to the field) |
| a card's entry area | `hexes` and `board`: the named entry hexes | An entry along the whole edge |
| `GameState.Exits` | `CapturedBy`, `Escort` | No prisoner, no escort |

The action `asl.game.advance` no longer requires `to`, and takes `exit`. Existing games replay unchanged.
