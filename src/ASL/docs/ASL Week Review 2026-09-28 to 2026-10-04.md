**How this bears on your Platoon and Company Simulation Design.** By its sections:

- **Sections 3, 7, and 13.** The design names the planner's bounded projection cache as a foundation. It is pass 31d's, built the day the design is dated. Section 7's rule for a checkpoint, that replay from it must equal replay from the beginning, is what pass 31d's tests check on two played games. The fault that pass removed (15 whole-game replays a check; 8 seconds to confirm by Turn 5) is the measured case for section 13: a new domain's projector should continue from a kept state from its first day.
- **Section 8.1 and conformance case 9** (nothing hidden may leak through a preview, a reason, or a list of legal actions). This is the fault the week met most often: 13 of the 15 passes from 23 to 31d. Rulings R31c.4, R31c.7, and R31d.5 are worked answers to "which reasons may a side read". - **Section 14.5, Resolve in ASL.** A child engagement, in ASL's terms, is a scenario card: sides, OB groups, setup areas, entry, turns, and Victory Conditions as data (passes 17 to 22 and 28), and a setup plan is the form a `DeploymentRecord` would take (passes 30 and 30b). Two things are missing. No card is made by a program; cards are authored. And a finished game yields a result and VP, not a record of survivors, positions, and conditions: an `EngagementOutcome` needs that export.
- **Section 14.11's example** has a tank immobilized. ASL has two tank types and no Immobilization from To Kill before pass 33. A child engagement of Infantry alone is within reach now; one with armor waits on passes 32 and 33.

The play tests give its cost: five turns of The Guards Counterattack took 190 proposals and 0:26 by script, eight of The Tractor Works 273 proposals and 1:15; the first test, driven by a browser agent, took 2:14. A child played by people is a sitting.
- **Section 14.4** (a change of scale changes no one's knowledge). Free LOS checks (R31d.4) is the one standing ASL ruling that gives a side more than the table does. A linked engagement's information policy would have to name it.
