Narrative decisions
- Interviews occur after both sides have set up, immediately before play.
- Germany sets up first; the Soviets see that visible setup before placing their
  forces and move first.
- The presenter supplies a complete overview using the Adjudicator perspective.
- Characters know their own deployment, the public scenario card, and the opposing
  top counters. They do not claim unrestricted inspection of enemy stacks.
- Public single-hex building assignments can support location deductions.
- Building assignments come from the card; placements inside multihex buildings
  are the particular arrangement selected for guards-map-tour-01.
- The saved deployment has no units marked Hidden or Concealed. Question marks
  shown before play represent uninspected counters under the top of enemy stacks.
- Preserve named personnel and paragraph-level national-accent directions.
- Spoken script contains no commentary about the document revision process.
