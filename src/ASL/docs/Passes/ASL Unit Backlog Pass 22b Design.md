# ASL Unit Backlog Pass 22b Design

**Status:** Built. Pass 22b (the theme and the shared foundation) of the [ASL Card Play and Map Studio Redesign Plan](<../ASL Card Play and Map Studio Redesign Plan.md>), its first pass.

**Date:** 2026-09-30

**Related documents:** the [Scenario A1 Backlog Pass 22b Review](<Scenario A1 Backlog Pass 22b Review 2026-09-30.md>), the plan's sections 10, 11, 13.6, 15, and 16 (the component catalog), and the [ASL Unit Backlog](<../ASL Unit Backlog.md>), section 34.

The pass adds no rule, ruling, counter, or package. It cites R0.3, R17.2, R18.1, R22.3, R22.4, and A26.4, all ruled by earlier passes.

## 1. Outcome

- **The theme (22b.1).** Branch `UI-Redesign-01` is merged: `wwwroot/site.css` with the supplied tokens, the local fonts, and the viewport's data attributes; `app.css` is retired. A shared `.toolbar` rule makes every page's row of controls a wrapping flex row, and `.toolbar-note` puts a note on its own line in one. The Unit Lab's starting-template list no longer runs into the preview column, and its previews scroll inside their column (the plan had placed this in 22d.4).
- **The shell (22b.2).** S01 `StudioNavigation` groups the destinations (Boards, Maps, Units, Scenarios, Play, Verify, Settings), marks the current group for nested routes too, and hides when collapsed. `MainLayout` owns the toggle: a collapsible sidebar when wide, a drawer under 1024px (`studioShell.js` reports the width; no styles are written from script). Escape anywhere in the shell closes the drawer and returns focus to the toggle; a route change closes it and does the same; a route never changes the wide sidebar. S02 `PageHeader` is on ten pages; the board editor and viewer get theirs with their workspaces (22d.2, 22d.3). The source-derived draft restriction is a warning banner.
- **The shared components (22b.3).** In `Components/Shared`: S03 `SourceRequiredNotice`, S04 `OperationFeedback` (a status region that is always present and empty when silent, so screen readers announce what is put in it; a failure inside it is an alert), S05 `StatusBadge`, S06 `FindingList` (severity in words, an explicit empty state), S07 `FieldGroup`, S09 `PerspectivePicker`, and S10 `RevisionNavigator`. S09 and S10 are adopted by tasks 23.1 and 22c.3. The card editor's findings use S06, and its note uses S04 with the outcome set with each note.
- **The card picker and the new game (22b.4).** K02 `CardPicker` is the same on Play, Scenarios, and the card editor, marking the user's cards "(yours)". K01 `NewGameFromCard` holds the card, the game id and label, the start, and the Balance. K03 `CardStartSummary` gives the boards; the Game Turns (with a last half turn), the side that sets up first, and the side that moves first or that a dr decides; each side's ELR, SAN, and FBE; the place and date as the card prints them; the SSRs the game enforces, with the number the card prints; and the Scenario Defender. K04 `BalanceChoice` offers a side's Balance by agreement, or, when both players wish to play one side, a dr whose loser plays the other side with its Balance; each side's Balance text is shown under the choice.
- **Card provenance (22b.5).** `CardProvenance` in the Play library builds a prepared model from the card, the catalog, and the pass 17 comparison registry, which the Play csproj embeds. K21 `CardProvenancePanel` shows it beside the card on Scenarios and in Play's card panel: the identity (id, built in or the user's file, SHA-256, format, pinned catalog); for a game, the card id, hash, and catalog it started from and whether the card still matches; the source, legacy card, and adaptation notes ("not recorded" when blank); each side's counters apart, with Balance counters on rows of their own marked "Balance only (A26.4)", each with its counter reference, source record (a manufactured counter says its values are manufactured under R0.3), and its printed values by plain name with sheet, counter, face, and row; and each citation as a registered fragment (physical PDF page and the comparison in words, the fragment id as the title) or a ruling linked to section 5 of the Backlog Passes Plan. A card with no OB counters says so. Play builds the game's model again only when the card, its hash, or the game changes; when the card is gone, Play lists what the game recorded.

## 2. Tests

`SharedComponentTests` (MapStudio): route groups, the navigation's current group and hiding, the shell (collapse when wide, the drawer, Escape, a route change), the page header, the finding list, the status region, the field group, the source notice, the perspective picker, the revision navigator, the card picker, and the Balance choice. `CardProvenanceTests` (Play): every citation of the three built-in cards resolves; a fragment's page and an unknown rule; counters' sources and manufactured values; each side's counters and Balance counters apart; a game's match. Page assertions in `ScenarioCardsPageTests`, `ScenarioCardStartPageTests` (the Game Turns line, the Balance wording and texts, FBE), `CardEditorPageTests`, and `TablePlayer22Tests`.
