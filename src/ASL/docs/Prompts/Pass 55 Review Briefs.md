# Pass 55: the five reviews' briefs

Pass 55 (Armored combat II) is built on `feature/asl-backlog-pass-55`, cut from main at `846eb5a3`. Its design is `src/ASL/docs/Passes/ASL Unit Backlog Pass 55 Design.md`: sections 1 to 6 are the frame with rulings R55.1 to R55.21; section 7 (the Depletion Number's notation and four counters), 8 (fire at another level), 9 (special ammunition), 10 (Canister), 11 (Armor Leaders and Inexperienced Crews), 12 (Motion attempts and the DEFENDER's Motion firer), 13 (Gun Duels) say what was built, each beginning with what departs from the frame. Rulings R55.22 on are in those sections.

## Said in every brief

- Read-only. Do not edit any file, build, run tests, start a server, or run a git command that changes state. Do not create a worktree. An untracked `.codex/` folder at the repository root is not the pass's.
- The pass is `git diff 846eb5a3..HEAD -- <path>`, run as `git -C E:/Archive/GitHub/dlandi/LimboDancer.MCP diff ...`. Never `cd` into the repository, never a heredoc.
- Search with the Grep and Read tools; never `grep -r` in a shell over `src/ASL` (it walks `bin` and `obj`).
- The rulebook's page texts are in `E:\Archive\GitHub\dlandi\pass32-tools\pass35\session3\pages\pNNN.txt` (physical pages 043 to 253); other pages with `python E:/Archive/GitHub/dlandi/pass32-tools/pass55/dump_pages.py <page>` into `pass55/pages/`. A rule is read on its page file, never from memory. A rule's example is part of the rule. Quote at most one phrase of under 15 words for a finding.
- No file is written: the findings are the closing message. At most 12, most serious first, each with a grade (BLOCKER, SHOULD FIX, NOTE), the file and line, the rule and its page, what is wrong, and the smallest fix. Then one paragraph on what was read and what could not be checked.
- What the design lists as "left out", "not built", or a departure is not a finding. A finding is a case the code reaches that the rule does not cover, a case of the rule left out that the design does not list, or a plain fault.
- The catalog's ordnance: the German 7.5cm INF Gun (`attacker-inf-gun`, 75*, H7), the Russian 45L AT Gun (`defender-at-gun`), the British 6-Pounder (`british-at-gun`, 57L, NT, ROF 3, `HE7^3m2 HE8^4+ D6^4m6E D7^5`), the American 37mm AT Gun (`american-at-gun`, 37LL, NT, ROF 2, `C7^2m8+`); the PzKpfw IIIH (`attacker-tank`: T turret, 50, ROF 2), the T-34 M41 (`defender-tank`: RST turret, 76L, no ROF, red MP), the SPW 251/1 (`attacker-halftrack`: MA AAMG, OT); light mortars, a PSK, a PF, two ATRs. No vehicle has Canister, a Gyrostabilizer, or AA capability beyond the halftrack's AAMG: a rule for one of those is not a finding.
- A fact this pass adds to a record is absent from a record made before the pass, which must still check as it was recorded. A place where an older record would now fail, or resolve differently, is a BLOCKER. So is a place where a side's view shows what that side may not know.

## 4. The Rules boundary

The user's ruling: every ASL rule is a calculator in `LimboDancer.Domains.Asl.Rules` that takes facts and returns a verdict; LOS and range stay in Maps, read by Play and handed over as facts; Units references Rules so the projector calls the same calculators; Rules references only Abstractions; the Studio decides no rule. The diff in Rules, Play, Units, Maps, and the Studio.

- **Asked, in the added or changed lines only:** a rule decided in Play, Units, or the Studio (a DRM computed, a "may" or "must" decided, an exception tested), as against fact gathering and event writing. Look hardest at `GamePlanner.Canister.cs` (which Locations are attacked, the elevation test, what refuses the shot), the three places that pass over a Canister fire record for A7.55 (the design's section 10.1 names them: say whether one Rules member can take them), `GamePlanner.MotionAttempts.cs` (which mover is answered, the Bog Checks' count and order), `GamePlanner.GunDuels.cs` (what makes a return shot void, what is waived for the duel shot), `GamePlanner.Ordnance.cs` and `GamePlanner.Fire.cs` (levels, cliffs, Height Advantage), `LiveOrdnance`, `LiveFire`, the projector's `KeepArmorLeaders` (when he is revealed and lost) and `GameView` (what the enemy sees), `GamePlanner.IsArmedAfv`, and the Studio's `Play.razor` and components (the ammunition list, the Canister aims, the Motion attempt block, the placement form). Also: a second copy of a decision that Rules already makes; a planner check and a projector check of one rule that can disagree; a new fact that is not absent-safe.
