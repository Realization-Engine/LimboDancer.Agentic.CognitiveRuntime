## 1. Outcome

- **A side's view (task 23.1; R23.1, R23.3).** `GameView.Of` takes the OB groups setting up now (`GamePlanner.OutOfSight`: during a card's setup, the enemy groups of the order setting up) and leaves their units out of a side's view entirely (A12.12); a group already seen stays seen. Before play starts, each enemy stack not under "?" shows its top counter, the first listed, and the rest become `SealedPresence`s marked `Uninspected`, with the equipment beneath them withheld (A2.9); a fortification is a top counter, since its units are beneath it. From the start of play the enemy's Known units are seen whole. Concealed enemy counters stay "?", a Dummy always, and hidden ones absent. A side's events leave out the setup events that created what it may not see. `GameLibrary.ViewOf` serves the Play map, the board viewer, and the Games page alike. `GameLibrary.Projection` passes the out-of-sight sides, so the Play map and the board viewer read the same view. `UnitDocument.Uninspected` names such a counter in the accessible name and the inspector.
- **The hand-over screen (task 23.1; R23.2).** `HandOverScreen` wraps everything below the Play toolbar. A change in the "View as" picker sets the view waiting; until its viewer confirms ("I am the russian side: show my view", focused), the map and the panels are not rendered at all, so nothing of the last view stays in the page. The pending proposal, the LOS line, the highlighted hex, and every draft of setup and of the actions are dropped; the CC declarations stay, since both sides declare them openly into one proposal. Focus is not moved, since an arrow key in the picker fires its change; Tab reaches the confirm button next. Choosing the shown view again drops the hand-over. Opening a game shows its phasing side's view, as before.
- **The board link (section 15.4; R23.3).** P02 `LiveGameToolbar` builds "View on bd01" with the page's view; it is left out while a hand-over waits. The adjudicator link appears only for the adjudicator's view.
- **Hidden sequential setup (task 23.2; R23.3).** The user chose the rulebook's reading over the plan's first text: the side setting up is out of the other's sight; once its order has finished, the other side sees its stacks' top counters and counts. The setup section says which side sets up out of the viewer's sight. A minimal card has no order, so its setup stays on the honor system.
- **HIP by SSR (task 23.3; R23.5).** `ScenarioSetup.Check` reads the card's SSR tokens `hip:<side>:<n>`: up to n squad-equivalents of MMC hidden, the SMC hidden only with a hidden MMC of their Location, Infantry only (a hidden Gun or vehicle is refused), in Concealment Terrain only, never a Dummy or a unit also under "?". The card check refuses a token naming no side of the card. `GamePlanner.NightAndWeatherRulesBar` refuses a malformed token, so the card editor and a new game both refuse it. A hidden unit is placed beneath "?" by the new action `asl.game.place-hidden` (event type `hidden-placed`, seen by its side), from the start of play, whenever its side chooses; the move of a hidden unit is refused with that message (A12.32). The Play page shows a "Propose: place ... beneath ?" button for each of the viewer's hidden units.
- **The non-OB "?" (task 23.4; R23.6).** The setup action takes `conceal` (a side and its Locations) once every group that sets up on board has set up and before play starts: each stack with no "?", no hidden unit, and no broken or berserk unit, 17 hexes or more from every unbroken enemy ground unit or out of its LOS within 16 hexes (`GamePlanner.NonObConcealment` lists them), gains "?" by `setup-concealed` events, which are setup events, seen by the side, and recorded in `GameState.NonObConcealed` so the OB allotment does not count them. The range is read first; a LOS the board cannot decide refuses the stack; a refusal names no enemy Location. The Play page offers the qualifying Locations as check boxes for the viewer's side.
- **Control as a side knows it (R23.4).** `ScenarioVictory.Evaluate` and `GamePlanner.Victory` take `knownTo`: the enemy's concealed and hidden units then neither gain nor prevent Control, event by event, nor count among its unbroken squad-equivalents (A26.15). K08 `VictoryStandingTable` shows a side's standing with a note, and the "if the game ended now" line only to the adjudicator. After the game ends, both read the recorded standing.
- **Play components (task 23.5).** First, component tests pin the Victory standing in a side's and the adjudicator's view. Then: P01 `ScriptedDicePanel`, P02 `LiveGameToolbar`, P07 `GameReplayFailure`, N02 `NightWeatherSummary`, R01 `ProposalReviewPanel` with R02 `EntryReviewFacts` and R03 `FireReviewFacts` as its evidence, R12 `PlayUnitTable` (rows in words, never the state), R13 `AdjudicatorAuditPanel` (it receives the lines only for the adjudicator), K05 `SetupPoolsTable`, K06 `PlayCardPanel` with K07 `GameEndNotice` and K08, and `HandOverScreen`. The page's view, its card setup, the sides out of sight, and the Victory standing are read once per history, revision, and viewer. The unit, weapon, CC, and entry pickers read the view; a side with no replayed view sees nothing. The Move panel is shown to the moving side and the Fire panel to the firing side (and both to the adjudicator); the other side reads a line saying who acts. K05 shows a group setting up out of sight by its status only.

## 2. Event format

- `setup-concealed` (new): `{ "id": "<unit>" }`, a setup event; the unit gains "?" and is recorded as a non-OB "?".
- `hidden-placed` (new type, payload of `conditions-changed`): the unit loses hidden status and gains "?".
- The setup action's schema gains `conceal` (an object); the new action `asl.game.place-hidden` takes `unitIds`.

## 3. Tests

- `BacklogPass23Tests` (Play, 10): the groups setting up out of sight and their creation events left out, the top counter and the counted ones, Known units whole once play starts; HIP refused without its SSR; the allowance, a lone hidden leader, the hidden unit unseen, placed beneath "?" only in play, and its event unseen by the enemy; a malformed token; the non-OB "?" refused before both have set up and where LOS is not decided, placed on a stack 22 hexes away on a minimal card, not twice, and play starting after it; with a LOS stub, a clear LOS within 16 hexes refusing and a blocked one allowing, a broken enemy and a Dummy not counting, a broken unit never gaining "?", and a Dummy always "?" to the enemy; Infantry only for HIP and a token naming a card side; Control as each side knows it.
- `StateTests` (Units): the German stack's top counter and two counted ones before play.
- `PlayComponentTests` (MapStudio): the standing in both views, the hand-over leaving its content out, the unit table's "?" rows, the audit only when given. `PlayPageTests.ANewViewWaitsForItsViewer` (the hand-over on the page and the board link) and `AHandOverDropsTheLastViewsDrafts` (a setup draft gone, the movers the moving side's own). Five page tests now hand the screen to the firing side before firing, as players do. The Play page tests change the view through `PlayViews.ViewAs`, which confirms the hand-over.
