# R2, referee, Riders to wrecks (65.4 to 65.9): the review's findings (2026-10-24), as reported

Checked and correct: the eight vehicle crews against R65.3; the Rider dates and capacities; the Carrier capacity and its HS; the start dr on the colored die; no Survival after an Unconfirmed Kill (C7.42; the design's section 4 was wrong to list it as missing).

1. MAJOR. Riders do not Bail Out when a fire changes the TCA. `GamePlanner.Riders.StepBailOut` (Riders.cs:27) is called from movement steps only (Vehicles.cs:429, :602); an ordnance shot's turret turn (Ordnance.cs:252, :418), Canister (Canister.cs:143), CMG fire with a turret turn (LiveFire.cs:1188), and a Motion attempt's `turret` (MotionAttempts.cs:167) turn it without. D6.21 (p. 205). Fix: call `ScenarioA1RiderRules.TurretBailOut` from each and append `AddBailOuts` after the turn (or refuse a turret turn while Riders are aboard).
2. MINOR. A Carrier's crew may not Remove its MG (`ScenarioA1CrewActsRules.MgRemoval` :79, `vehicleClass != "halftrack"`). D5.41 (p. 204) cites 6.83 for Carriers; D6.83 (p. 206); D6.8. Fix: accept class carrier (D6.83 still forbids Removal on the quick-unload path).
3. MINOR. A crew leaving its vehicle loses its +1 or its Armor Leader on three paths. D5.34 (p. 203), D3.43 (p. 200). `TaskCheckAbandons` (Ordnance.cs:1232) passes the leader but not `stunRecovery`; the Carrier crew unload (`PlanUnload`, Passengers.cs:383) passes neither; Survival (Ordnance.cs:1202, Fire.cs:1252) passes no leader (D3.43 has no Survival exception; only D5.341's Recall eliminates him); a crew inside leaving by `instance-moved` (CrewLeaves, Ordnance.cs:1298) never takes the vehicle's +1. Fix: pass `Is(vehicle, StunRecovery)` and `vehicle.ArmorLeader` on every non-Recall path; move the +1 on the `instance-moved` path as `Abandon` does.
4. MINOR. Survivors placed by `instance-moved` keep their MF (`SurvivorEvents` PassengerTargets.cs:127; crew-inside survival Ordnance.cs:1192, Fire.cs:1241; the TC exit Ordnance.cs:1235). D5.5, D5.6 (p. 204), D6.9 (p. 207): placed "expending all remaining MF". Fix: end the unit's movement and spend its MF for these placements.
5. MINOR. The TC's second cause (ArmorCalculator :813-815, `ScenarioA1CrewSurvivalRules.SecondCause` :61): (a) the MG To Kill path (A9.61) runs through the same `ResultOn`, so a MG hit can trigger it, though a MG is not ordnance; (b) a Possible Shock whose NTC passes "fails to ... Shock", but the filter excludes it. D5.5 (p. 204). Fix: gate on `!machineGun`; evaluate after the Possible Shock check when `Shocked != true`.
6. MINOR. The Survival DRM collapses two +1s into one (`SurvivalDrm` :18): D5.6's +1 for broken or Shock/Stun, and D5.34's separate +1 counter. Fix: `(broken || stunned || shocked ? 1 : 0) + (stunRecovery ? 1 : 0)`, gated on the prc fact.
7. MINOR. A General Collateral Attack reaches the absent crew of an Abandoned Carrier (FireCalculator vehicle effects :2506), and of an Abandoned OT AFV (older). Fix: skip the crew attack when `vehicle.Prc is not null && vehicle.Abandoned == true`.
8. MINOR. A Shocked crew may Abandon (`AbandonBar` :35; `MayAbandon`, `PlanAbandon` CrewActs.cs:49, :85). C7.42 (p. 177). Fix: a Shocked, Unconfirmed Kill, or Stunned bar.
9. MINOR. Riders may board or set up in terrain that bars them (`PlanLoad` Passengers.cs:325; setup `RiderBar` Vehicles.cs:123). B13.43 (p. 128), D6.21. Fix: refuse `riders: true` and Rider setup where `TerrainBailOut`'s prohibited terrain holds the Location.
10. MINOR. The Scrounging dr ignores leadership (`PlanScrounge` Scrounging.cs:93; `ScenarioA1WreckRules.Succeeds`). D10.5 (p. 211) "1-3 dr (∆)"; A.10 (p. 43): ∆ takes the Leadership DRM. Fix: the best Good Order leader's modifier in the Location as a drm, recorded on `vehicle-scrounged`.
11. MINOR. A Wounded SMC Temporary crew is Wounded again instead of eliminated (`SmcCrewOutcome` :143; `GameProjector.SmcCrewAfter` :4091). D5.343, A17.2. Fix: pass `alreadyWounded`; eliminated when both.
12. MINOR (boundary). "Captured" decided in Play from nationality (`PlanRecrew` CrewActs.cs:231). Fix: Rules decides from a recorded fact (a prior `vehicle-captured`, or the vehicle's original side).
13. NOTE. A broken Rider's NMC can read "pinned" (`ScenarioA1RiderRules.Check` :151). Fix: `Passed` for a broken unit at its morale.
14. NOTE. A missing Rider definition gives morale 0 (`AddBailOuts` Riders.cs:101). Fix: refuse with `definition-incomplete`.
15. NOTE. The Carrier exception reads current towing (`InherentHalfSquad` Ordnance.cs:1448); D6.82 says "specified as towing ordnance". Backlog row.

Boundary: finding 12; `VehicleMovedThisPlayerTurn`/`VehicleFiredThisPlayerTurn` (Ordnance.cs:1440-1445); "turreted" from MA-type literals (Riders.cs:38) and a stream by name (:53).
