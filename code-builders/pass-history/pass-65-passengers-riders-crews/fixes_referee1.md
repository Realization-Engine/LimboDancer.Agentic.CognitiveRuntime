# R1, referee, Passengers (65.1 to 65.3 and 2k): the review's findings (2026-10-24), as reported

1. MAJOR. A loader is eliminated with a vehicle the loading DFF destroys. GamePlanner.Fire.cs about 1044-1054 (the D5.43 follow-up), PassengerTargets.cs:36,54 (`OnFoot`), `ScenarioA1PassengerCalculator.LoadFailures`. D5.43 and its EX (p. 204): a loader fails "if ... pinned, eliminated or loses its Good Order Status (or the vehicle is destroyed)". The load step put them aboard; `OnFoot` keeps them out of the PRC fact (no Survival); `LoadFailures` returns only pinned or broken; `KeepPassengers` then eliminates the unharmed loaders with the destroyed vehicle. Fix: `LoadFailures` also returns every surviving loader when the vehicle's effect is Eliminated or BurningWreck, their `instance-moved` written before the wreck events.
2. MAJOR. Riders lose their Specific Collateral Attack when the hit Shocks or Stuns the AFV. ArmorCalculator about 652 (`standing` excludes Shock, Stun, `kill.Shocked` unless a Carrier); FireCalculator FT/MOL/DC about 1893 the same; AreaCalculator:318-320 (a struck AFV joins the IFT attack only on None or Immobilized). D.8 (p. 192) and the Collateral Attack Table note 1 (p. 193): "If crew/Passengers are/become stunned or shocked, Collateral Attack applies only to the vehicle's Riders." Fix: Shock and Stun stop only the crew's attack; still call `SpecificOnPrc` (`Exposure` already spares Passengers when the crew is impaired).
3. MAJOR. Hazardous Movement's -2 (and Mud's cushion) reaches PRC. FireCalculator.TargetOwn (2325-2327) and `Own` in VehicleEffects (2347-2349), whose drm feeds `WithPrc`. A4.62 (p. 51) is for the units moving hazardously; D.6 and D.8B modify an attack on PRC as if they were its only target. "hazardous-movement" is not target-only, so every Vulnerable PRC in the Location (and the Vehicle line) take -2, now common with R65.21's survivors; "weather-cushion" (E3.62, +1 TEM) reaches exposed Passengers on top of their CE DRM (D5.31 forbids). Fix: strip both from the PRC's DRM, gated on the prc fact.
4. MAJOR. A Rider fires at 1/8 in the AFPh after its AFV moved. `ScenarioA1PassengerActionRules.Multipliers` (98-103) plus "advancing-fire" at FireCalculator 3993-3996. D6.22 (p. 205) "quartered if the vehicle has moved or is in Motion"; D6.63 (p. 206) treats the movement halving and the AFPh halving as one. 1/2 x 1/2 x 1/2; `MountedPreview` says 1/4. Fix: no Rider movement halving in the AFPh where Advancing Fire already halves, or one D6.22 quarter.
5. MINOR. A Shocked AFV's Passengers can still be attacked: `WithPrc` calls `Exposure(..., vehicle.Stunned == true)` without `FireVehicle.Shocked` (there since 2h). D.8 (p. 192). Fix: `Stunned == true || Shocked == true`.
6. MINOR. BU Passengers of an OT AFV under Air Bursts are not attacked (`WithPrc`'s `reached`, the Air Burst branch about 2451). D5.311 (p. 203): Vulnerable even if BU. Fix: when `AirBurstUnarmored` holds, treat BU Passengers as reached with the exposed DRM.
7. MINOR. From an elevation beyond the range, the crew keeps a flat +2 while its Passengers' CE DRM is reduced (VehicleEffects `crewDrm` 2524-2529). D5.31, D6.61, D5.33. Fix: with the fact, `ScenarioA1PrcRules.CeDrm(beyond)` for an OT AFV's crew too; the "treated as unarmored" part stays with pass 105 (backlog row).
8. MINOR. A D5.311 crew that breaks leaves the AFV at once (Ordnance.cs `UnprotectedCrewEvents`, FireFollowUps `UnprotectedCrewPlacedBroken`). D5.311 (p. 203): the broken crew routs in the RtPh, placed beneath by spending its initial-RtPh MF; the vehicle Abandoned if the crew routs or is eliminated. Fix: keep the broken crew inside until the RtPh (the rout-beneath path), or record a ruling for the shortcut.
9. MINOR. A failed Immobilization TC cancels the PRC's Specific Collateral Attack (`kill.Abandoned != true` in `standing`, ArmorCalculator about 652, FireCalculator FT/DC). D.8A (p. 192): whenever the vehicle is not destroyed. Fix: Abandonment stops only the crew's attack.
10. NOTE. Open PRC lose Height Advantage in a Location with AFV or wreck cover (FireCalculator 3411 adds HA only when `Cover(attack) is null`; `TargetOwn` strips "afv-cover" from PRC). B10.31 (p. 125). Fix: read HA for PRC apart from the Infantry's cover.
11. NOTE. `OverrunShare` (line 215) gives 1/2 to the Passengers of any CE armored vehicle; D7.11 (p. 207) gives 1/2 only to an armored halftrack (and a Carrier, D6.8). Harmless today. Fix: key it on the vehicle class.

Boundary items (MINOR), the same as R5's: `PassengersExposed`, `MovedThisPhase`, the Rider's moved choice, the OVR Passenger filter, Shocked counted as Shocked or UK, `PrcOf`'s null for a concealed or hidden vehicle.
