1. **MINOR: attacks called by a Sniper's own LLMC come too late, and so does the Sniper Check.** `AddSniperAttacks` :46-71 reads LLMC triggers only in the next loop, after the whole batch; `KeepTriggers` :90-93 appends them after `rest`; `Snipe` :600-607 then :621-640 pends the Sniper Check right after the LLMC, before the chained attacks; `PlanSniperAnswer` :775-778 runs `rest` before the wrapper reads the resumed attack's LLMC. A14.3 (p. 82): an attack "is considered resolved only after the full resolution of all further Sniper attacks generated by ... all LLMC/LLTC"; A14.4: the check comes "After resolving (14.3) an effective Sniper attack"; A14.1: a later trigger waits for "all Sniper attacks made prior to it". With step triggers T1 and T2, T1's attack eliminating a leader whose LLMC calls L: the rule's order is T1, L, T1's check, T2; the build runs T1, the check, T2, L. Argues against R71.13's "each resolved after the attacks before it". Fix: after `AddLeaderLossChecks` in `Snipe`, resolve that attack's own LLMC triggers (or put them at the head of `rest`) before offering the check; `KeepTriggers` prepends.
2. **MINOR: a SMC Temporary crew of an unarmored vehicle or Partially Armored AFV is broken or pinned, not eliminated or wounded.** `SnipeVehicle` :936-938, :958-963 (`UnprotectedCrewEvents` breaks the `CrewInside` unit); `ScenarioA1Sniper.CrewResult` :485-488. A14.3: dr 1 "Eliminates SMC ... breaks Inherent crew of unarmored-vehicle/Partially-Armored-AFV", dr 2 "Wounds SMC ... pins ... Inherent crew"; a Temporary crew (A21.22) is not an Inherent crew; D5.343 covers the fully armored case (built in 4f). Fix: a SMC crew of a vehicle not fully armored goes through `UnitEffect(smc: true, ...)` and `SnipeUnit`'s eliminate and wound paths.
3. **MINOR (hidden information): a pending Sniper Check proves a concealed stack holds a real, Good Order, unfired unit** (:623-640; the public `choice-pending` written only when `SniperCheckers` finds an eligible unit). A12.14, A14.23: the Sniper player learns only the declared count; a declined check tells the opponent nothing at the table. Fix: when any unit of the attacked side in the Location is concealed, always pend the choice there (a "take" with none eligible does nothing), or owner-only with a neutral wait. (R5's finding 11, the same.)
