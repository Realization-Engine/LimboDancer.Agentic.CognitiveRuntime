## Build (rulings R71.3 to R71.8; design section 5, items 2 to 4)
1. **Eligibility (R71.3, R71.4).** One Rules member deciding who is a Sniper target from facts Play reads through the index (`MemoOf(state).Objects(state)`: `Location`, `VehicleOf`, `Aboard`, `CrewOf`): a unit inside a vehicle only if Vulnerable (a PRC whose vehicle is CE or, for a Carrier, whose Passengers are exposed, or Partially Armored, or unarmored; any Rider); the Inherent crew (the vehicle as a "crew" candidate) when the vehicle is CE, Partially Armored, or unarmored; an unarmored vehicle with PRC as a "vehicle" candidate; an empty unarmored vehicle ineligible (R71.4); an AFV with nothing Vulnerable no target. Use `ScenarioA1PrcRules.Exposure` and `ScenarioA1FireEligibility.CrewExposed` rather than a new reading of CE. The Partially Armored trait (`asl:partially-armored`) becomes a definition property read like `Unarmored`. A14.33: a vehicle and its PRC never by the same dr (they are separate candidates).
2. **Results (R71.5).** dr 1: Recall the CE crew of a fully armored AFV; break the Inherent crew of an unarmored vehicle or Partially Armored AFV (pass 65's `UnprotectedCrewEvents(state, vehicle, attemptId, broken: true)` or its closest form); immobilize an unarmored vehicle (no effect if already immobilized). dr 2: Stun the CE crew of a fully armored AFV (Recall instead when it is already under Stun +1, D5.342); pin that Inherent crew. Stun, Recall, Immobilization through the existing `VehicleConditions` writer so the projector's handling applies. New `sniper-attacked` result strings (stunned, recalled, crew-broken, crew-pinned, immobilized). A Stun or Recall on a vehicle manned by a SMC Temporary crew goes through the projector's `SmcCrewAfter` as today (4f adds its Severity dr; do not change it here).
3. **The enemy Sniper as a target (R71.6).** The enemy `asl:sniper` entity counts in the Alternate Target hex search (its hex's TEM the lowest Location's), is part of every Location of its hex, and is a Random Selection candidate; dr 1 eliminates it (`instance-eliminated`; the SAN reduction is 4b's, leave a clear seam), dr 2 pins it (`conditions-changed` Pinned on the entity). Results `sniper-eliminated`, `sniper-pinned`. The projector clears a Sniper's pin with the CCPh's other pins (GameProjector.cs:670-672 clears units and equipment only).
4. **SMOKE in the TEM comparison (R71.7).** Each eligible target's TEM plus the in-hex SMOKE DRM (`SmokeSources`, `ScenarioA1VehicleSightRules.SmokeDrm`), the lowest taken.
5. **A12.14 (R71.8).** An effective attack on a concealed unit costs its "?" when it is in the LOS of a Good Order enemy ground unit (pass 70's sight table, `ScenarioA1Sight`, the Loss purpose, through `GamePlanner.Sight.cs`), whatever the result; a break, wound, Reduction, or elimination costs it always. Today `PinnedConditions` always strips it and the no-effect case never does.
6. **The leak (section 5, item 2).** A record of an attack that leaves its unit concealed names no unit (`SnipeUnit`'s None branch, Snipers.cs:290-292).
7. **The index (section 5, item 4).** `Snipe` must not replay the history four times an attack nor read `state.Location` per unit: take the state once and read the index.
8. **The record lines (section 5, item 3).** `PlayRecords.SniperRecords` and the Replay step name the hex in brackets and the unit "[G4]: 4-6-7 squad G1" as the page's other lines do (`SayAt` / `PlaceOfUnit`), with the new results worded and cited (A14.3, A14.31, A14.33, D5.34, D5.341).
