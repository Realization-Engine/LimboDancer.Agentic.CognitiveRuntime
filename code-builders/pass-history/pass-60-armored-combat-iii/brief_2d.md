# Increment 2d: FT, MOL, and DC at an AFV (task 60.3)

Read `brief_build.md` beside this file first; it binds you. The rulings are R60.2, R60.3, R60.15, and R60.16 of the design (section 6, F2, F3, F7). Increments 2a to 2c are committed before you start; 2b's `ScenarioA1ArmorCalculator.SpecificCollateral` and the shared crew outcome (`ScenarioA1FireCalculator.CrewChecks`, `CrewOutcome`) are what a FT's, MOL's, or DC's attack on the CE crew uses.

## Pages
p. 55 (A7.307), p. 89 and 90 (A22.3, A22.34, A22.5, A22.6 to A22.612 and the example on p. 90), p. 91 (A23.3 to A23.6), p. 176 (C7.21 and its examples), p. 177 (C7.344, C7.346, C7.4 to C7.7), p. 190 (the C7.34 and C7.7 charts; their values below, read on a render), p. 192 and 193 (D.8, D.8A, the Collateral Attack Table), p. 198 (D2.41), p. 199 (D3.2).

**The values (R60.2), as Rules tables with their page:** C7.34 Basic TK#: DC 16; FT 8; MOL 6. AF applies to the DC only ("[AF NA]" covers FT and MOL). FT: halved at Long Range (halve first, then add), +1 CE and +2 OT (both if both). MOL: +1 if CE and closed-topped, or +2 if OT (never both); -2 against a moving target (C.8); +1 or +2 for an elevation advantage. Both, and the DC: +1 through the rear Target Facing (C7.21). C7.7, the DC column: at most half the Final TK# burns; below it eliminates; equal, and one above, Immobilize a hull hit or Shock a turret hit. The FT/MOL column: below the Final TK# burns (at most half too); equal eliminates (Crew Survival); one above, nothing. An Original 12 is a Dud. Note A (red CS#) is left out: the catalog has no color.

## Build
1. **Predesignation (R60.15):** the fire request gains an optional named vehicle for a FT, MOL, or DC attack (an argument, absent on every older request). Only an AFV in the target Location may be named; with none named, the attack is as now (and a MOL or DC with any vehicle in the Location stays refused, as now, unless one is named).
2. **FT at an AFV (A22.34, C7.344):** with the AFV named, the FT's attack is a To Kill DR on the FT column: TK# 8 (halved at Long Range first), +1 CE, +2 OT, +1 through the rear hull Target Facing (the hexside crossed, from the VCA; the rear if from the same hex), no AF, nothing added to the DR. Results on the FT/MOL column. If the AFV survives, its CE crew takes a Specific Collateral Attack at the FT's IFT FP (24, 12 at Long Range) on the same Original DR, +2 for CE. No other unit of the Location is attacked. The FT's own rules that exist stay (fuel on an Original 10 or more, A22.5; no TEM).
3. **MOL at an AFV (A22.612):** with the AFV named and the MOL Check passed, one DR is the To Kill DR on the MOL column (TK# 6 with its modifiers above, -2 against a moving vehicle by the existing moving-target fact, +1 through the rear), the Specific Collateral Attack on the CE crew at the Small Arms FP plus 4 (+2 CE), and an IFT attack on every other unarmored unit of the Location at the Small Arms FP alone. A failed Check is the IFT attack without the 4 on every unarmored unit, the CE crew as a General Collateral Attack. A colored 6 breaks the user and cancels the MOL as now. The Flame is not built (pass 185). A MOL at an unarmored vehicle stays refused (backlog).
4. **DC at an AFV (A23.5, C7.346):** with the AFV named, a Placed (after the existing PAATC) or Thrown DC rolls the Position DR with the modifiers whose facts exist: +2 if the AFV is not Stopped, in Motion, or concealed (once); +2 Thrown; +1 through the hull front; +1 CE; +1 Thrown in the AFPh not by an Opportunity Firer; +1 CX; -1 through the hull rear; -2 Immobile; -2 OT; Case B's -1/-2 only if elevation is a fact of the DC's attack; the Bypass -2 only if Bypass is. A colored dr below the white is a turret hit (C3.9). Final 5 or less: the Aerial AF (find how D1.6 or the counter gives one; if no value exists, take the lowest printed AF of the hull and say so); 6 to 8: the AF of the facing; 9 to 11: no effect on the AFV, the CE crew takes a Specific Collateral Attack on a new DR; 12 or more: the AFV and its crew untouched, the Location's other unarmored units attacked as Area Fire. An Original Position DR of 12 is the DC's malfunction (no detonation). The TK# is 16 less the AF, +1 through the rear; results on the DC column; each Immobilization brings the existing Crew Task Check. A Position DR of 8 or less on a surviving AFV: its CE crew takes a Specific Collateral Attack at 30 FP on the same DR. The thrower's own Location is still attacked at +3 by its own DR (A23.6).
5. **D2.41:** the DC's +2 is the Position DRM above; the MOL's is its -2 on the TK#; neither stacks.
6. **The page:** the fire panel lets the player name the AFV for a FT, MOL, or DC (only when the attack holds one of them and the Location holds an AFV the side sees); the proposal states the TK# and its modifiers; the record line gives the To Kill DR and its result, and the Position DR for a DC.

## Gating (R60.3)
Every attack here was refused before the pass or did not name a vehicle (a FT's IFT attack on a Location with an AFV attacked only the CE crew, and still does when no AFV is named). No record changes; say how you checked.

## Tests
Rules: the FT's TK# (8, halved at Long Range, +1 CE, +2 OT, +1 rear) and each row of the FT/MOL column; the MOL's three attacks on one DR, its failed Check, its -2 against a moving AFV; the DC's Position DR bands with a few modifiers, its TK# with the AF of the band, the DC column's rows, the Specific Collateral Attack after each. Play: a FT at a CE AFV, a MOL at an AFV beside a squad, a Placed DC at an AFV; each replays clean.
