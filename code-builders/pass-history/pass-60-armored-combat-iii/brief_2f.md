## Build
1. **A MG's To Kill attack on an AFV (A9.6, A9.61, A9.611; R60.19).** The cheapest faithful path is to let a MG ride the ordnance shot: `asl.game.fire-ordnance` with the MG as the weapon and a named AFV as the target vehicle, the ammunition `ap`. Read how far the ordnance shot's stations take a MG (the To Hit, ROF, malfunction, fire counter, Acquisition, the projector's test of a possessed weapon) and build what is missing; if a new action is cleaner, say why. Conditions: a SW MG fired alone (no Fire Group, no other weapon of its possessor), at a named AFV within the MG's Normal Range, in the PFPh or DFPh (the MPh's Defensive First Fire too if the ordnance shot handles it); not in the AFPh (backlog). The To Hit DR on the Vehicle Target Type with black numbers and the ordnance DRM, the MG's ROF as ordnance, its malfunction at its B# on the Original To Hit DR; no Critical Hit, no Multiple Hit. The hit Location by C3.9 (a colored dr below the white on a turreted AFV hits the turret), the facing by the hexside crossed (D3.2, the colored dr from the same hex). The To Kill DR: Basic TK# 4 (12.7mm and 15mm by their rows if any catalog MG is one), Case A +1 through the rear, Case D's 25mm row by range, less the AF of the facing; at most half the Final TK# burns, below it eliminates, equal Stuns (even BU, D5.34), above nothing; no Shock, no Immobilization; an Original 12 is a Dud. An unarmored facing or an unarmored vehicle stays the IFT's Vehicle line (as now). If the AFV survives, its CE crew takes the Specific Collateral Attack at the MG's IFT FP on the same DR (A9.611; the D1.22 example). The MG keeps its ROF as ordnance; its Residual FP: backlog.
2. **A vehicle's MA MG (D3.54).** A vehicle whose main armament is a MG (the SPW 251/1's AAMG `aamg`, the Bren Carrier's `mg`) may make the same attack with it; its BMG and CMG may not. The Bren Carrier's MA `mg` is not read by the vehicle fire path today (it takes `aamg` only): repair that for both its IFT fire and this attack (the frame's section 5, item 3).
3. **A Fire Lane and CC Reaction Fire (A9.223, D7.21; R60.20).** A Fire Lane is cancelled when its MG's possessor fires CC Reaction Fire (the narrow reading: no cancellation on entry alone). D7.21's CC counter bars the unit's SW and Guns, not only the unit, from Non-CC Reaction Fire at the vehicle in its Location. Read what pass 35 and pass 45 built and change only this.
4. **The upper superstructure (C3.9, D1.6; R60.22), only if the rest is done and time is left:** a colored dr below the white on a non-turreted AFV hits its upper superstructure, with the AF D1.6 gives it (the printed AF unless the counter shows otherwise, which the catalog does not), gated by a fact on the record so that an older hull hit resolves as it did. If not built, say so: it moves to pass 105.

## Gating (R60.3)
Items 1 and 2 were refused (a MG's attack on an AFV attacked only its crew; no MG fired as ordnance): no record changes. Item 2's Bren Carrier repair admits fire that was refused. Item 3 changes what a recorded CC Reaction Fire does to a Fire Lane: check whether any recorded game holds both, and gate it if so. Item 4 is gated.
