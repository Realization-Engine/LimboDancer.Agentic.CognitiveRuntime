# Fixes from the first referee (R1: tasks 60.1, 60.2, 60.4), as judged by the session

Ruling R60.24 (amends R60.11, decided under the user's word of 2026-10-15, from C3.33 p. 169 and the C3.331 example p. 170): a mortar's hit also attacks the target hex's units and vehicles that are out of the firer's (or Spotter's) LOS when the shot hit the non-hidden enemy target in its LOS that was the hardest to hit (the highest net To Hit DRM), on the same Effects DR.

Fix:
1. MAJOR (R60.24). `ScenarioA1AreaCalculator.Run` (about lines 85-86) drops the targets and vehicles of a Location whose LOS is blocked. Keep the out-of-LOS Locations in `AreaLocations`; when the in-LOS enemy target with the highest net To Hit DRM is hit, attack them on the same Effects DR with their own TEM and levels above.
2. MAJOR. A Gun crew's `gunshield:` and `emplacement:` DRM reach the vehicles of its Location (`ScenarioA1FireCalculator.TargetOwn` about 2167, `VehicleEffects` about 2187 and 2201): count them as the crew's own (C11.5: "the Good Order manning crew (only)").
3. MAJOR. `ScenarioA1OrdnanceCalculator.Run` (fate about 1220-1226): a Critical Hit whose Random Selection picks an unarmored vehicle still destroys the Gun. "Destroyed" only when the selection picked the crew (or, for an unattended Gun, the Gun), as `ScenarioA1AreaCalculator` does (`picked.Any(item => item.Id == gunCrew)`).
4. MAJOR (hidden information). `GamePlanner.OrdnanceSummary` (Ordnance.cs about 192) gives each concealed or hidden target its own entry, and a hidden unit alone makes the shot "mixed": the firer learns a HIP unit exists and how many units are under a "?". Leave Hidden units out of the text and out of what makes the preview "mixed"; give a Location's concealed units one entry ("its concealed units, on an Original DR of N"). The needed Original DR comes from Rules (the boundary review's item 5).
5. A Critical Hit on an unarmored vehicle on the Infantry Target Type reverses none of its positive TEM (`VehicleEffects` about 2201, `critical-hit-tem:*` dropped as target-own): keep the vehicle's own reversed Case Q on its line (C3.71, Height Advantage included).
6. A PF or PSK at Personnel uses the Infantry Target Type's Critical Hit test (`Run` about 1062-1075 and the mixed branch): on a LATW's To Hit table an Original 2 is a Critical Hit outright (C3.7, p. 170, with its bracketed exception); use that rule when the weapon has its own To Hit table or is a PF.
7. A non-20mm ATR gives a 1 FP Specific Collateral Attack (`SpecificCollateral` about 649, through `ScenarioA1HeEquivalency.Firepower`): C8.31 note 1 and C13.24 give an ATR HE Equivalency only if it is 20mm; return none for the Collateral Attack of any other ATR (its 1 FP at a Gun, C13.23 and R60.6, stays).
8. `AreaAcquisition` is taken from the record unchecked (`WithRecordedMapFacts` about 524): the same as the boundary review's item 6; one fix.

Not fixed now (backlog rows, and the design's section 9.1): `vehicles-beyond-kia` also refuses a mortar's shot at a hex with more vehicles than the column's highest KIA# (C1.55 limits the vehicles affected, it does not bar the shot); a tie in the Critical Hit's selection between Locations goes to the lowest (A.9 settles ties among units, not Locations).
