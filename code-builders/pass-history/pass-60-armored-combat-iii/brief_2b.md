Read `brief_build.md` beside this file first; it binds you. The rulings are R60.3, R60.7, R60.8, and R60.9 of the design (section 6, F3 and F5). Increment 2a (HE Equivalency, committed before you start) gives the IFT FP of AP and HEAT.

## Build
1. **The Specific Collateral Attack (D.8A; R60.7).** After a Vehicle Target Type ordnance hit on an AFV that does not destroy it (the TK DR missed, an Immobilization, an Unconfirmed Kill, a Stun of the vehicle is not a Shock), its CE crew (Vulnerable: not BU, not stunned or shocked, per `VehicleEffects`' own test) takes an IFT attack at the round's IFT FP (the HE FP of an HE round by its caliber; AP, APCR, APDS, HEAT by 2a's HE Equivalency) on the Original TK DR, with the CE DRM alone (no TEM, Hindrance, FFMO, leadership). Its effects are those of the IFT's CE crew effects that exist (`VehicleEffects`: KIA/K Recall, MC/NMC/PTC crew check). None after a Dud (Original 12), a Shock this hit causes (C7.42), a destroyed vehicle (eliminated or burning), a buttoned-up crew. One attack for a Multiple Hit; no Residual FP. Passengers and Riders: pass 65. The result is recorded inside the ordnance resolution and shown in the record line ("its CE crew takes a Specific Collateral Attack of 6 FP on the same DR, +2 for CE (D.8A): NMC").
2. **Gating (R60.3).** A Vehicle Target Type hit could be made before the pass. The attack happens only when the shot's facts carry the pass's fact (for example `specificCollateral: true`), set by the planner on every new shot and taken from the record at replay (with the map facts, not recomputed). A record without it resolves as before. Say in the report how you checked that an older recorded hit on a CE vehicle replays unchanged.
3. **The Infantry Target Type at a Location that holds a vehicle (C3.32, C3.41; R60.8).** Lift `play.ordnance-vehicle` when the shot names no vehicle and the Location also holds Infantry or an unarmored vehicle. Each unarmored vehicle is a target hit on its own Final TH# (its size as Case P, its own TEM), and every unit and unarmored vehicle hit shares the one Effects DR on the HE (or equivalency) FP column: the unarmored vehicle on the Vehicle line (A7.308, the A7.309 Unlikely Kill, at most as many vehicles as the column's highest KIA#), its Vulnerable PRC as `VehicleEffects` does after an IFT attack. An AFV in the Location is immune; its CE crew takes a General Collateral Attack on the same DR, with its own DRM, when the AFV's own Final TH# (its size, its TEM) is reached. A Location that holds only AFVs stays the Vehicle Target Type's: keep the refusal there, worded so. Random Selection of a Critical Hit (C3.74) takes the unarmored vehicles into its pool; a CH on a vehicle goes to it, and the units it attacks Collaterally are attacked normally.
4. **Known and concealed units together (C3.4; R60.9).** Lift `concealment-mixed`: one To Hit DR judged per unit, Case K +2 for a concealed one; every unit hit takes the same Effects DR at the same column; a concealed unit hit loses its "?" as the existing concealment loss after a hit does (A12.14), one missed keeps it. Units out of the firer's LOS or too close stay as they are handled now; say what that is.
5. **The page:** the target list already offers the Location; check that a Location with a vehicle and Infantry can be chosen with no vehicle named, and that the proposal's preview says which units and vehicles the DR would hit.

## Gating
Items 3 and 4 were refused before the pass: no record changes. Item 1 is gated (item 2).
