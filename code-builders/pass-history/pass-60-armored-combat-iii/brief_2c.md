Read `brief_build.md` beside this file first; it binds you. The rulings are R60.3 and R60.10 to R60.14 of the design (section 6, F3 and F6). ## Build
1. **A mortar's hit on a vehicle (C1.55, C3.332, C7.345, C9.1; R60.10).** Lift `play.ordnance-area-vehicle`. A vehicle in the target hex is a target of the Area Target Type like the units (each judged on its own To Hit as the Area calculator judges units, with the vehicle's size). On a hit: an unarmored vehicle on the Vehicle line (A7.308, A7.309); an AFV on the IFT at half the HE FP (the Area halving), never on a To Kill table, with -1 if every AF is 4 or less, -1 if OT, +1 if every AF is 8 or more, and the TEM that does not give it HD; a Final KIA destroys it (Crew Survival as the existing kill does), a DR at most half the K/# DR leaves a burning wreck, the K/# DR and the DR one above it Shock a turret hit or Immobilize a hull hit (the hit's Location from the To Hit DR's colored and white dice, C3.9), an MC or PTC does nothing to the AFV. At most as many vehicles as the column's highest KIA#. The AFV's Vulnerable crew takes a General Collateral Attack on the same DR with its own DRM, without the AF and OT modifiers. A Critical Hit on an AFV (C3.72) is at double the full FP with no TEM, the AF and OT modifiers kept. Infantry in the hex keep D9.3's +1 beside a friendly AFV, as the C9.5 example has it (check that the Area path reads it).
2. **A building of several levels (B23.32; R60.11).** Lift `play.ordnance-area-levels` for a building hex that is not an Interior Building Hex (if the board data cannot tell an Interior hex, take every building hex as not Interior and say so; a rooftop is not attacked). Each Location of the hex that holds a target and is in the firer's (or spotter's) LOS is judged on its own To Hit modifiers (its own Hindrance); one Effects DR attacks every Location hit, each with its building TEM plus +1 for each level above it (not for a rooftop). A Critical Hit goes to one Location by Random Selection (C3.74), then to one unit there. A Location out of LOS is not attacked. Friendly units in the target hex stay refused; give `area-friendly-units` its sentence in RefusalReasons (frame, section 5).
3. **Air Bursts in full (B13.3, D5.311; R60.12).** The -1 of an Area hit in woods applies to an unarmored, CE, or OT target (the CE crew's Collateral Attack included: +2 CE -1), combined with an entrenchment or Emplacement TEM, kept on a Critical Hit (not reversed). An OT AFV hit in woods by Air Bursts is attacked as unarmored (the Vehicle line) with no Air Burst TEM on the vehicle. A closed-top CE AFV takes nothing from it; only its crew does. None against a unit in Bypass.
4. **Area Acquisition by hex (C6.521; R60.13):** a mortar's Area Acquisition covers every Location of the hex in its LOS (today it is keyed to one Location).
5. **The gunshield's +1 against Indirect Fire (C11.4, C11.5; R60.14):** a crewed Gun in the target hex of a mortar's hit takes +1, not +2, from its gunshield; check whether a mortar's hit on a Gun is refused today (`target-outside`, R9.3) and, if so, admit it with this +1 and C11.4's fate (a KIA destroys the Gun, a K malfunctions it).
6. **A Critical Hit's reversal of Height Advantage (C3.71; R60.14):** on a Direct Fire Critical Hit on the Infantry Target Type, a target that claimed Height Advantage takes -1 for it. This changes an attack that could be made before the pass: gate it (R60.3) with a fact set on new shots and taken from the record.
