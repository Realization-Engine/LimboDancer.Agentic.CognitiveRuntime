# Increment 2e: Guns as targets, and Random SW and Gun Destruction (task 60.5)

Read `brief_build.md` beside this file first; it binds you. The rulings are R60.3, R60.17, and R60.18 of the design (section 6, F3 and F8). Increments 2a to 2d are committed before you start: 2a gave AP, HEAT, and an ATR at a Gun; 2c a mortar's hit on a Gun with the gunshield's +1; reuse them.

## Pages
p. 65 (A9.74, A9.8), p. 178 (C8.42), p. 181 (C11.2), p. 182 (C11.3, C11.4, C11.5, C11.51, C11.52, C11.6 and its notes 1 to 5), p. 170 (C3.41), and wherever a KIA is resolved (A7.3, p. 55).

**C11.6 (R60.2), as a Rules table with its page:** by the Final DR before the gunshield's DRM. Ordnance, Bomb, OBA: KIA or lower eliminates the Gun; K is Malfunction and Casualty Reduction (eliminates if AP fired, note 5); a Critical Hit eliminates. MG, IFE, Small Arms, FT, MOL, OVR: a KIA is Random SW/Gun Destruction (A9.74); K and CH are NA. DC: KIA eliminates; K is Malfunction and Casualty Reduction. Note 1: a Gun in tow dies only with its vehicle. Note 3: with Personnel in the hex, an unpossessed Gun checks A9.74 on an Indirect Fire KIA.

## Build
1. **Random SW and Gun Destruction (A9.74, C8.42, A9.8; R60.17).** On every Final KIA of an IFT attack (Infantry fire, a vehicle's MG, Residual FP, a FT, the IFT effects of an ordnance hit, Canister), each SW and each Gun held (possessed, or manned) by a unit that KIA eliminated rolls a dr, read as an IFT DR on the attack's column with no DRM: a KIA destroys it; a K malfunctions it, or eliminates it if it was already malfunctioned or has an X# (a dm SW counts as a SW). The Gun that an ordnance, DC, or OBA attack targets is C11.4's, not A9.74's. A unit eliminated by a K or by Casualty Reduction is not a KIA. Unpossessed weapons checked by Indirect Fire or an OVR, the OVR's end-of-MPh check and its -1, and an OVR of an empty Location: backlog. Find every place a KIA eliminates a unit (the codemap's IFT, ordnance hit, Canister, Residual FP, vehicle MG, FT paths) and where `GameProjector.DropHeldBy` drops the held weapons; the destruction happens before the drop, as part of the attack's events (an `instance-eliminated` of the weapon, or its malfunction).
2. **Gating (R60.3):** this changes what a recorded KIA does. The new dr is applied only where the attack's record carries the pass's fact (for example `randomDestruction: true` on the fire attack and on the ordnance shot), set on every new attack and taken from the record at replay, never recomputed. A record without it destroys nothing, as before. Say how you checked that an older recorded KIA on a squad holding a MG replays unchanged (a test that strips the fact, as 2b's `AnOlderRecordOfAHitOnACeTankReplaysAsItWas` does).
3. **An unattended Gun as a target (C3.41, C11.4; R60.18).** The Infantry and Area Target Types may name a Location whose only target is an unmanned Gun (no crew, not in tow): a hit's IFT DR destroys it on a KIA and malfunctions it on a K (C11.6's ordnance column), with no gunshield and no Emplacement. Today `GunAt` needs a manned Gun and the shot is `target-outside`. On the page the target list offers that Location.
4. **The Gun Destruction Table's other column (C11.51, C11.6):** an IFT attack by a MG, small arms, a FT, or a MOL never destroys a Gun but through item 1. The DC column: a DC's attack on a Location with a manned Gun destroys it on a KIA and malfunctions it on a K. Check what a DC does to a Gun today and say so.
5. **Emplacement's limits (C11.2, C11.3):** a Gun manned by a non-crew unit loses its Emplacement for the rest of the game (a fact of the state, set on the first such manning; check what `UnemplacedGuns` holds now); a Gun on a paved road or a bridge is never Emplaced if the map tells those (else skip and say so); a Gun manned by a squad gets no +2. The Area Target Type's option (Target Size as a To Hit DRM and +2 TEM once hit) is 2c's: check it holds.

## Gating
Items 1 and 5 can change a recorded attack: gate each (item 2; for item 5, a recorded shot keeps the Emplacement its record holds, which the verifier already recomputes: check, and take it from the record if needed). Items 3 and 4 were refused or did nothing before.

## Tests
Rules: the A9.74 dr on each row (KIA destroys, K malfunctions, K on a malfunctioned MG eliminates), none on a K or a Casualty Reduction, none without the fact; a Canister KIA destroying a SW; an unattended Gun destroyed and malfunctioned; a DC at a manned Gun; a non-crew manning ending Emplacement. Play: an IFT KIA on a squad holding a LMG (the dr, the LMG destroyed), an older record without the fact replaying unchanged, a shot at an unattended Gun; each replays clean.
