# Increment 2a: HE Equivalency (task 60.4, and C11.52 and C13.23 of 60.5)

Read `brief_build.md` beside this file first; it binds you. The rulings are R60.2, R60.3, R60.4, R60.5, and R60.6 of the design (section 6, F2 to F4).

## Pages
p. 178 (C8.31 and its table, read on a render: AP below 37mm 1; AP 37mm and up 2; every APCR, APDS, and ATR 1 (footnote 2: the below-37mm column takes all APCR, APDS, and ATR, no MG); BAZ and PIAT 8; PSK, PFk, and H#[9] 12; PF 16; other ordnance HEAT one IFT column left of the Gun's normal HE FP; footnote 1: an ATR uses it only if 20mm; footnote 4: PF, PFk, H#[9] leave no Residual FP), p. 169 (C3.32), p. 182 (C11.4, C11.52, C11.6 and its note 5), p. 183 (C13.23, C13.31), p. 185 (C13.9), p. 221 (the PF chart), and A8.2 on p. 60 for Residual FP.

## Build
1. **The HE Equivalency table** as a small Rules table with its page (R60.2): a function from the ammunition, the Gun's caliber and HE FP, and the weapon kind (Gun, PF, PSK, ATR) to the equivalency FP, or null where none applies. HEAT of a Gun: one IFT column left of its printed HE FP; a Gun with no printed HE FP (no HE) has none, and its HEAT is refused at Infantry.
2. **AP, APCR, APDS, and HEAT at an unarmored target on the Infantry Target Type** (C3.32, C8.31; R60.4). Lift `gun-outside` and `he-equivalency-not-built` for a named ammunition at a target with no vehicle, where the Gun lists that ammunition at the date (the Depletion Number steps of pass 55 still apply to APCR, APDS, HEAT, and AP by number). The shot runs the HE path that exists (TEM as Case Q, one IFT DR on the equivalency FP for every unit hit, a Critical Hit doubling it and reversing positive TEM). HEAT is refused at Infantry unless the target Location gives a wall, building, rubble, or pillbox TEM (a new refusal with the rule). None of these leaves Residual FP (if ordnance leaves none anywhere today, say so and add nothing). An unnamed ammunition at Infantry stays HE, as now. The page's ammunition list offers what may now be fired at the chosen target, with the equivalency in the note ("AP: 2 FP by HE Equivalency, C8.31").
3. **The ATR (R60.4):** the catalog's two ATRs are not 20L: they keep firing at Personnel as Small Arms on the IFT; nothing changes there.
4. **A PF and a PSK at Personnel (R60.5):** lift `target-type-outside` for a PF or PSK at a target with no vehicle, under the HEAT bar of item 2 (wall, building, rubble, pillbox TEM). PF 16 FP, PSK 12 FP, on the IFT, with the To Hit they use now. The PF Check takes +1 at a target that is not an AFV, and +1 in August and September 1943. A PF's hit affects one unit by Random Selection (C13.31); a PSK's every unit it hits. No Residual FP. Backblast (C13.8) unchanged.
5. **AP, HEAT, and an ATR at a Gun (R60.6; C11.52, C13.23):** an ordnance shot of AP, APCR, APDS, HEAT, or an ATR at a Location where a crew mans a Gun (the `GunTarget` path that HE uses) resolves at the equivalency FP, and the Gun's fate is C11.4's with C11.6's note 5: a K eliminates the Gun and its crew when AP (or APCR, APDS, ATR) is fired; with HEAT a K is Malfunction and Casualty Reduction as with HE. An ATR (any caliber) at a Gun attacks at 1 FP and, unless it is 20L, only the Gun and its manning crew.
6. **The game-file library** `code-builders/test-games/game_file.py` now defaults to catalog 1.17.0: use it for any test game you write (write test games only as Play tests; do not write live game files, I do).

## Gating (R60.3)
Everything here was refused before the pass, so no recorded attack changes; say so in the report after checking that no recorded shot could take a new path (an unnamed ammunition at Infantry stays HE; an AP or HEAT named at Infantry was refused).
