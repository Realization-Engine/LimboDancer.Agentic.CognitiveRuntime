# Fixes from review 1 (the referee: ammunition and Canister). Paths under src/ASL/. Verify each line number before editing.

Sniper Checks on a Canister shot depend on the path. `LimboDancer.Domains.Asl.Play/GamePlanner.Canister.cs` about :253-267 (`Build`) never calls `AddSniperAttacks`; but when one Location stops at an owner's choice the resume goes through the "fire" case of `GamePlanner.Choices.cs` about :183-191, which does call it over the resumed and remaining Canister records. R3 (FIX). The adjacent hex is attacked when no cluster Location is. `GamePlanner.Canister.cs` about :161-199 and `CanisterAims` about :75. C8.41 (p. 178): "if firing at a same-level target, it attacks units in the hex adjacent to the firer along the LOS". An aim whose three cluster hexes are off the map, at another level, or out of LOS still attacks the adjacent hex and spends the round, and `CanisterAims` offers an aim that has only its adjacent hex on the map. Require at least one attacked cluster Location before adding the adjacent ones (otherwise the existing empty-aim refusal), and offer only aims with a cluster hex on the map. The decision belongs in Rules (`ScenarioA1CanisterCalculator`): a member that takes the cluster's and the adjacent hexes' facts and returns what is attacked or the refusal.

R5 (FIX). AP or APDS named at Infantry by the 6-Pounder is refused with the wrong reason. `ScenarioA1OrdnanceCalculator`, the C2.21 block: any ammunition other than `he` gets `he-not-named`. Give `he-not-named` only when `shot.Ammunition` is null, and otherwise a reason that says AP against Infantry is C8.31's HE Equivalency, which is not built.
