North African transport evidence

Approximate town-to-town connections, not traced historical alignments. No distances, capacities, crossings or travel times may be inferred. Display tiers express our presentation choices, not historical railway classes.

By 1939 means existence evidence, not verified service on 1 September. Later evidence is displayed only when enabled. Route dates refer to the evidence used, not necessarily opening dates.

Tripolitanian local railways, Egyptian Delta branches and Suez connections still need route-level review.

A continent-wide prewar railway GIS of matching European detail has not been acquired for North Africa.

Post-1939 evidence is optional and is not backdated. Changes in gauge, wartime destruction and capacity are not modeled.

Source findings

Railway Wonders of the World: In Northern Africa (1935): Contemporary account of the Maghreb trunk and selected branches. Intermediate points are schematic, not traced alignments.

SNCFT historical chronology: Operator dates northern and southern Tunisian openings; Heidra to Kasserine opened in 1940 and is omitted from the prewar view.

ONCF 2013 annual report, historical timeline: Timeline dates Casablanca-Rabat to 1925, Oujda-Bouarfa to 1931 and Benguerir-Safi to 1936.

Railway Wonders of the World: In the Nile Valley (1935): Contemporary Egyptian network account. Cairo-Aswan and Alexandria-Cairo are represented as schematic corridors.

British official history: Mediterranean and Middle East I, chapter IV: Pages 67-68 document road and single-track railway supply to Matruh at the outbreak of war. Water supply constrained railway operations.

US Army: Northwest Africa, chapter II: Pages 18-20 describe the 1942 road system, inland links and railways. Some roads had recent military improvements; this is not proof of 1939 condition.

Milan civic historical collections: Pariani papers: Archive catalog identifies March 1937 coastal-road inauguration documents at Bardia, Arae Philenorum and Misurata. Catalog evidence, not surveyed road geometry.

Wavell dispatch: operations December 1940-February 1941: Dispatch identifies Benghazi railway junction and terminals at Barce and Soluch. This review establishes 1941 existence only.

British official history: Mediterranean and Middle East I, chapter VI: The 1940 account distinguishes the coast road from desert tracks, including Trigh Capuzzo. Road and track are separate classes.
