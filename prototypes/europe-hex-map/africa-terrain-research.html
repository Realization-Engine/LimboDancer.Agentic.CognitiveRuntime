Southern Tunisia: chotts and wadis

1943: The official history describes the chotts as shallow salt-crusted basins and identifies wadi barriers around Mareth. Salt basins are not treated as permanent open water; seasonal passability is unknown.

Western Desert: plateau, scrub and Qattara

1940: The British history describes a rocky plateau, coastal escarpment, low scrub, scarce water and the Qattara Depression. Desert is not uniformly sand or woodland. Qattara and escarpment limits still need digitization.
