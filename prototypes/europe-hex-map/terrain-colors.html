The continuous demo landscape combines every supported base type and feature: wooded hills, village roads, a stream crossing, marshy shores, bocage fields and desert terrain. Select a feature on the edited map, or choose a color below. Edits stay in this utility until approved for campaign use.

What is being edited?

Colors and grid opacity only. Hex types, elevations, roads, streams and building geometry are unchanged. Defaults use your approved exported terrain palette. Restore defaults returns to those colors. Additional terrain symbols are illustrative preview artwork, not implemented campaign rules. Both previews share the same zoom and position.

Counter colors come from your approved export, not unsaved changes in Counter Colors. Click terrain in the edited preview to select its color. Drag samples to move them in both previews; Space + left drag or middle drag pans even over a counter. Positions are retained per board during this visit; Reset counters restores their starting positions. Samples are illustrative, not historical deployments. Amber remains the reserved selection color.
